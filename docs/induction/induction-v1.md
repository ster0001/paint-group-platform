# Paint Group Contractor Induction

> Export of the Claude Doc "Paint Group Contractor Induction", rev 16, 13 October 2026. The doc is the wording authority; this file is what the induction loader reads. Re-export to this path after any edit to the doc.

The induction turns onboarding into a signed-off session where new contractors watch each step on the big screen, do it on their phone in the demo property, and tick it off. Thirteen short modules, about three hours with a break, in the contractor's own language.

## How the induction runs

Every module has the same shape: three to five slides on the big screen, then "On your phone" in the demo property, then a tick in the app. Painters learn by doing, so nobody reads a document.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Who runs it | Tom or the Project Coordinator, in person, one to three new contractors at a time |
| Where | Big screen for the slides, each contractor on their own phone, Wi-Fi |
| How long | About three hours with one break. Can split into two sessions: modules 1 to 9, then 10 to 13 |
| Language | The contractor picks their language on the first screen: English, Dari (Afghan), Farsi, Arabic (Lebanese), Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) or Italian. The wording they read and tick to confirm understanding is in that language, including the standards. The slides, the app and the demo job stay in English |
| Paid | No. The invite says so, so nobody asks on the day |
| The demo property | 12 Demo Street, a copy on every contractor's account. Same property, same planted events, so the slides always match the phone. Nothing in it counts toward their light or their money. "Reset demo" puts it back to the start |
| The check | No written test. Before they leave, the facilitator watches each contractor do five things without help: accept a job, add a before photo, move a surface to prepped, raise a variation, submit an invoice |
| The kit (facilitator only) | Roll of painter's tape, the laminated site card, and a sample board if one exists. The board is never mentioned on the slides |

**Slide rules.** One idea per slide. Short sentences. A picture wherever a picture will do. Every module ends with an "On your phone" slide that names the exact taps, numbered, in the order the demo expects them. Say "Paint Group" or "your Project Coordinator", never a person's name.

## Modules 1 to 3: welcome, how a job runs, in a customer's home

### Module 1 · Welcome and your account (10 min)

**Slides**

1. **Welcome to Paint Group.** We find the work, quote it and look after the customer. You paint it to our standard. Three promises on every job: the customer always knows what is happening, the home is left clean, there are no surprises on price.
2. **What we need from you.** Public liability insurance. WorkCover if you have more than one worker. ABN and GST. Bank details. How many painters you bring.
3. **Haven't got your certificates with you?** That is fine today. The app shows what is missing and reminds you. Job offers start once your insurance is in.
4. **Choose your language.** What you read and tick to confirm is in your language, including the standards. The app and the demo job stay in English, with short words and pictures.

**On your phone**

1. Choose your language.
2. Fill in your details and the number of painters you bring.
3. Upload a certificate if you have it, or tap "I will add this later".

**Tick:** I have set up my account.

### Module 2 · How a Paint Group job runs (10 min)

**Slides**

1. **One picture, nine steps.** Offer → Accept → Pre-start (colours, materials) → Work days (updates in the app) → Pre-defect → Walk-through with the customer → Sign-off → Invoice → Paid.
2. **Who does what.** Paint Group quotes, books, orders materials, talks to the customer and pays you. You accept, paint, update the app, check your own work and run the walk-through.
3. **The walk-through is booked when the job is accepted.** It is on the last day. If the finish date changes, log it in the app the day you know, so we can tell the customer.

**On your phone**

1. Open 12 Demo Street.
2. Find the nine steps on the job screen. Which step is the job on now?

**Tick:** I know the nine steps of a job.

### Module 3 · In a customer's home (15 min)

**Slides**

1. **You are a guest in someone's home.** How you behave is half of what the customer remembers.
2. **Before you start:** shoes off until the floors are covered. Cover every floor fully. On hard floors, use the floor protection we provide and tape the edges to seal the area. Ask the customer where to park, where to leave materials, and whether you may use the toilet, power and water.
3. **While you work:** no smoking, no drugs, no alcohol. No eating inside an occupied home. Music only if the customer is happy with it, and ask first. Keep doors and windows you opened for ventilation locked at the end of the day. Keep pets and children away from wet paint and ladders. Don't move valuables; ask the customer to.
4. **Every day at the end:** materials packed in the agreed place and covered neatly. Rubbish stored neatly in one place, never scattered. Floors stay protected until the last coat is dry. Wet-paint signs where needed. Keys and alarm as agreed.
5. **Never:** wash brushes in the customer's sink or garden (agree a place). Take photos of the home except the work. Post anything online. Bring anyone who is not on your crew. Open a tin before checking the colour schedule.
6. **Arriving.** Arrive at the agreed time. If you cannot make it or will be late, tell Paint Group the day before. On the day, before 7:30 am. We tell the customer. Never leave the customer waiting with no message.

**On your phone**

1. Read the site rules in the induction and tick each one.

**Tick:** I will follow the site rules in every home.

## Modules 4 to 6: materials, finish standards, your status light

### Module 4 · Materials and equipment (10 min)

**Slides**

1. **We supply the paint and everything you use up.** Paint, filler, caulk, tape, drop sheets, floor protection, sandpaper, rollers and brushes.
2. **You bring the tools.** Ladders, spray equipment, pots, trays, poles, sanding equipment, power tools. Anything you do not use up.
3. **Where the materials are.** We deliver to site where we can. Where we cannot, we order to the nearest paint shop for you to collect. The work order shows which.
4. **Short of something?** Tell us in the app or call. We order more. Never leave a job waiting because something ran out.
5. **Colour matches.** We arrange them before the job where we can. Sometimes we need you to go to the paint shop. We set it up first, so the right materials are ordered for the agreed scope.
6. **Loan gear.** Where we have it, we lend spray equipment, big ladders and scaffold. Collect it and return it in the same condition.

**On your phone**

1. Open Materials on 12 Demo Street. See where they are being delivered.
2. Tap "Request more" on one item. Watch the reply come back.

**Tick:** I know what we supply and what I bring.

### Module 5 · Finish standards (20 min)

This module is the approved painter guide, Version 1, presented one section at a time. The six ticks here are the standards sign-off itself.

**Slides** (from the existing standards deck)

1. **The three levels.** Level 2 repairs what is broken. Level 3 fixes what a customer can see from 1.5 m. Level 4 fixes what a customer can see up close.
2. **Where the customer judges your work.** 3 m, 1.5 m, 0.5 m. The customer has the final say. Our quality checks use the same distances.
3. **How it is judged.** Face the surface. Normal light, no torch. High areas from the floor.
4. **Rules for every job.** No drips, no paint where it should not be, no paint on hardware, full cover, clean site every day.
5. **Your time.** Standard preparation is in your hours. Bogging, stain blocking and additional coats are extra time on the work order. Anything not allowed for is a variation.
6. **Interior surfaces**, then **exterior surfaces**, one surface per slide at the three levels. Show two or three on screen; they read the rest on their phone.

**On your phone**

1. Read and tick each of the six sections of the standards.
2. Open 12 Demo Street, tap Walls, tap "What we expect". See Level 3.
3. Stand 1.5 m from the nearest wall in the room and do the look test.

**Tick:** the six standards ticks, one per section.

### Module 6 · Quality checks and your status light (15 min)

**Slides**

1. **What a clean job is.** The app kept up to date from the reminder prompts. Every quality check passed first time. No call back in the 7 days after sign-off.
2. **Who gets checked.** New painters: the first 3 jobs. Yellow: 1 job in 3. Orange: every job. Green: only when we choose.
3. **The five colours.** New, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red, with how you get each one.
4. **What each colour gets you.** Green: priority on new jobs, paid within 3 business days of sign-off, bonus eligible. Yellow: normal. Orange: offers after Green and Yellow, a check on every job, a call from your Project Coordinator. **Red: no more job offers.** We meet with you about the finish on your current job.
5. **Everyone starts on New.** Four clean jobs in a row take you to Green. Lost it? Four clean jobs in a row win it back.
6. **This is a reward system.** It never takes money off your payment.

**On your phone**

1. Tap the light on your home screen. See New, 0 of 4.
2. A real text arrives now, marked DEMO: "Please update your job in the app today." Tap the link. Add a photo to the demo job. The prompt is marked answered.
3. Open "How the colours work".

**Tick:** I understand how my status works and what Red means.

## Modules 7 to 9: the customer, variations, pre-defect and walk-through

### Module 7 · Talking to the customer (15 min)

**Slides**

1. **Who talks about what.** You: hello on day one, what is happening today, simple questions about today's work, keep the site clean, update the app. Paint Group: price, scope, the level of finish, variations, colour changes, dates and walk-through bookings, complaints, call backs, invoices, customers who are not judging fairly, customers who need reassurance, requests for extras outside the scope.
2. **What the customer hears at each stage.** Booked: we confirm the dates. Day one: you say hello and explain the plan for the day. Work days: your app updates become their progress updates. Finish date change: you log it, we tell them. Last day: the walk-through. After: we send the invoice and the report.
3. **Running late or can't come.** Tell Paint Group the day before. On the day, before 7:30 am. We tell the customer. Never silence.
4. **Finish date changing?** Log it in the app the day you know. We update the customer and the walk-through booking.
5. **When a customer is unhappy.** Stay calm, listen, look at the area together from the right distance. Fix it now if it is our standard and quick. Anything else: no promises, take photos, note it in the app, call Paint Group the same day.
6. **Small extras are encouraged.** Never agree a price, a big extra or a change of scope on site. Say: "Good question. I will ask Paint Group to call you today."

**On your phone**

1. On 12 Demo Street, tap "Ask a question" and type anything. Watch the reply come back.
2. Tap "Change finish date", pick a day, give a reason. See it logged.

**Tick:** I know what I say to the customer and what Paint Group says.

### Module 8 · Variations (15 min)

**Slides**

1. **The scope is the job.** The work order lists every surface and the hours for it. Anything not itemised in the scope can be charged as a variation.
2. **Fair and reasonable.** Small items missed from the scope are part of the job, and what is small depends on the job. On a 2-day job, a missed window frame is a variation. On a 3-week job, 5 metres of fascia or 1 door in 50 is expected: do it. A whole room, a ceiling not in the scope or a second colour is always a variation. Not sure? Ask before you start it.
3. **Log it immediately.** The moment you see something missing or extra, log it. Not at the end of the job.
4. **The loop.** You log it with photos and hours → Paint Group prices it → the customer approves → it comes back to you to accept → it is added to your invoice. Never start the extra work before the customer has approved.
5. **What is never a variation.** Standard preparation. Paint applied too thin. Something you missed in your own work.

**On your phone**

1. On 12 Demo Street, find the extra door that is not in the scope.
2. Tap "Raise a variation", pick Extra item, add a photo and 1.5 hours.
3. Watch it come back priced and approved. Tap Accept. See it on the invoice.

**Tick:** I know what a variation is and how to log one.

### Module 9 · Pre-defect, walk-through and call backs (20 min)

**Slides**

1. **Why we pre-defect.** The customer should find nothing. Fewer defects at the walk-through, and the customer sees we are one step ahead.
2. **The tape check (jobs of 16 hours or more).** Finish painting and clean the site. Walk the job on your own and do the look test on every surface. Tape next to every defect. Fix each one and take the tape off. No tape left means ready.
3. **Before the walk-through.** Site clean, rubbish gone, floors uncovered, hardware back, windows and doors open and close, final photos in the app.
4. **How to walk the customer through.** Start at the front door. One room at a time. Let them look from the distance for the level. If they point at something small, fix it there and then. Anything bigger: note it in the app, no promises, Paint Group calls them. Then they sign on your phone.
5. **What a call back is.** A return visit on another day to fix workmanship. Fixed and signed the same day is a pass, not a call back. A customer call back counts for 7 days after sign-off. Only workmanship counts against you.

**Do it in the room:** the facilitator puts three pieces of tape on the wall. Each contractor finds them, explains what they would fix, and takes the tape off.

**On your phone**

1. On 12 Demo Street, complete the finish checklist and add a final photo.
2. Tap "Start walk-through". The demo customer flags one area. Tap "Fixed", then walk again.
3. Sign the demo sign-off.

**Tick:** I check my own work before every walk-through.

## Modules 10 to 13: the app day to day, money, calendar, safety

### Module 10 · The app day to day (20 min)

**Slides**

1. **A job offer lands.** It shows the address, the scope, the level, the hours and the money. Accept or decline within 24 hours. Accept means the dates are yours.
2. **Share the job with your crew.** Add your workers in the app. Each worker gets a login, does the worker induction, and then sees the jobs you share: scope, standards, photos and ticks. Invoices, variations and messages stay with you.
3. **Before photos first.** One photo of each area before you touch it, in good light, the whole wall. The app will not let you start an area without one.
4. **Stages.** Each surface goes To do → Prepped → Painted. Tick as you go. This is what the customer sees as progress.
5. **Keep it up to date.** Update on your first day, then every 2 to 3 days. We send reminder prompts. When a prompt comes in, update that day.
6. **Not sure about something?** Ask in the app. It goes to your Project Coordinator with the job attached.
7. **Rained off or no work today?** Tell Paint Group in the morning. We mark the day, and no prompt is sent.

**On your phone**

1. Accept the 12 Demo Street offer.
2. Add a worker: name and mobile. Share the demo job with them.
3. Add a before photo to Ceilings. Move Walls to Prepped, then Painted.
4. Tap "Ask a question" if you did not in Module 7.

**Tick:** I know how to run a job in the app.

### Module 11 · Money (10 min)

**Slides**

1. **Your price is on the offer.** Hours times the rate, fixed before you accept. Approved variations are added. No haggling, ever, with us or the customer.
2. **Invoice in the app.** After sign-off, tap Create invoice. It is already filled in from the job and the approved variations. Check it and submit.
3. **When you are paid.** Normal terms after sign-off. Green painters within 3 business days. A missing sign-off or missing before photos delays it.
4. **Expenses.** Parking, a top-up of materials we asked you to buy, tip fees: photo of the receipt, amount, category, job. Over the threshold, ask first. Approved expenses go on your next payment at cost.
5. **Bonuses.** Green painters are eligible. We tell you when one is added.

**On your phone**

1. On 12 Demo Street, tap Create invoice. Check the variation is on it. Submit.
2. Tap Expenses, add one: any photo, $20, Parking.

**Tick:** I know how to invoice and claim expenses.

### Module 12 · Calendar (5 min)

**Slides**

1. **Your calendar is how we offer you work.** Keep it true. Block out days you cannot work before we book you, not after.
2. **Link Google Calendar.** Your Paint Group jobs appear in your own calendar.

**On your phone**

1. Block out next Friday.
2. Link your Google Calendar.

**Tick:** My calendar is up to date and linked.

### Module 13 · Safety basics (10 min)

**Slides**

1. **Ladders and heights.** Footed, on a level base, three points of contact. Big ladders and scaffold: ask us, we lend them.
2. **Old homes.** Houses built before 1970 may have lead paint. Sheet walls and eaves may be asbestos. If you think you have found either: stop, do not sand or scrape, call us.
3. **Dust and fumes.** Sand with extraction or wet. Ventilate. Wet-paint signs where people walk.
4. **Your crew.** You are responsible for everyone you bring. They work to the same rules, and your light covers all of them. Every worker needs a white card.
5. **Something unsafe at the home?** Exposed wiring, a dangerous roof, an aggressive dog: stop and call Paint Group before you continue.

**On your phone**

1. Read the safety rules in the induction and tick each one.

**Tick:** I will work safely and so will my crew.

## Sign-off, the first three jobs, and the refresher

### The sign-off

Each module ends with one tick, "I have done this and I understand it". Module 5 holds the six standards ticks. When every tick is in, the app records the induction as complete, with the date, the language and the version, and emails the contractor a PDF of what they confirmed. The practical check is recorded by the facilitator on their own screen: five tasks, pass or not yet.

**Job offers start when:** the induction is complete, the practical check is passed, and insurance is on file.

### The first three jobs

1. **Day one of job one:** the Project Coordinator visits in the morning. Not a check, a hand-hold: photos, stages, where the materials are.
2. **Jobs one to three:** a quality check on each, as already ruled for new painters.
3. **After job three:** a 15-minute call. What was hard, what the app got wrong, what they would change.

### The 60-minute refresher for existing contractors

Existing contractors do not sit the full induction. They do the refresher, in person or on a video call, and the same ticks.

| Minutes | Module | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | What is changing (standards, call backs, the light) | Sets the scene |
| 10 | Module 3 · In a customer's home | The rules are now written down |
| 10 | Module 7 · Talking to the customer | Who says what |
| 10 | Module 8 · Variations | Fair and reasonable, log it immediately |
| 10 | Module 9 · Pre-defect and walk-through | The tape check |
| 10 | Module 6 · Your status light, plus the demo variation and walk-through on their phone | What Red means |

They also read and tick the six standards sections, as already ruled. Materials, money and calendar are a one-page handout, not taught.

### Keeping it current

When a rule changes in a material way, the changed module gets a "what changed" note and everyone re-ticks that module only. Small wording fixes do not trigger anything. Once a year, every contractor re-ticks the lot.

## The worker induction

Every painter on a Paint Group job does an induction, so every worker gets a login. A worker login is small: their induction, the jobs their contractor shares with them, photos and surface ticks. Invoices, variations, messages and the status light stay with the contractor.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Who runs it | Paint Group, in group sessions. The contractor may sit in but does not sign for their workers |
| How long | About 90 minutes |
| Modules | 2 How a job runs · 3 In a customer's home · 4 Materials · 5 Finish standards (the six ticks) · 6 What a clean job is and what counts (no light of their own) · 7 Talking to the customer · 8 Variations: tell your boss, never agree extras · 9 Pre-defect and walk-through · 10 Photos and stages only · 13 Safety |
| Not covered | Money, calendar, accepting offers, raising variations |
| The demo | Their own copy of 12 Demo Street with the worker steps only: open the job, add a before photo, move a surface to Prepped and Painted |
| The check | Three tasks without help: open the job, add a before photo, move a surface to Prepped |
| The gate | A contractor cannot put a worker on a job until that worker's induction is complete. PC Command shows who is not inducted |

The worker's ticks are in their own language too.

## The site card and the facilitator checklist

### The laminated site card (one page, for the van)

Front: **In every home**

1. Shoes off until the floors are covered. Cover every floor. Tape hard-floor protection.
2. Ask first: parking, toilet, power, water, where materials go.
3. No smoking, drugs or alcohol. No eating inside an occupied home. Music only if the customer is happy.
4. Keep pets and children away from wet paint and ladders.
5. End of day: materials packed in the agreed place and covered, rubbish in one place, doors and windows locked, keys and alarm as agreed.
6. Never wash brushes in the sink or garden. Never post photos of a home.
7. Check the colour schedule before you open a tin.
8. Late or can't come? Tell Paint Group the day before, or before 7:30 am on the day.

Back: **Before the walk-through**

1. Look test on every surface, from the distance for the level.
2. Tape every defect. Fix. Tape off.
3. Site clean, floors uncovered, hardware back, windows and doors open and close.
4. Final photos in the app. Then walk the customer through, front door first.

Footer: **Paint Group · Questions: your Project Coordinator · Standards: the Standards tab in the app**

### Facilitator checklist

**Before the day**

- Contractor's account created and the invite sent, with "the induction is unpaid" in the invite.
- 12 Demo Street is on their account. Open your own demo copy on the big screen and reset it.
- Phone with the DEMO text ready to send from the system.
- Kit: tape, site cards, sample board if there is one.

**On the day**

- Language chosen on each phone before Module 1.
- Run the modules in order. Do not skip the "On your phone" steps; they are the learning.
- Watch the progress screen. Nobody moves on until every phone has ticked.
- Module 9: the tape exercise on the wall.
- Practical check: the five tasks, without help, recorded on your screen.
- Hand over the site card.

**After the day**

- Confirm the induction shows complete and the PDF was emailed.
- Chase any missing certificates.
- Book the day-one visit for their first job.

## For Tom: assumptions and open points

Everything you ruled on 13 October is in. These are the calls I made on top, and the few things still open.

**Assumptions I made**

- Job offers wait for insurance even though the induction continues without it.
- Module 10 says accept or decline an offer within 24 hours, which is the SLA already in the scheduling build.
- Module 11 says "over the threshold, ask first" for expenses, which is the $100 pre-approval rule in the cost-capture brief.
- Safety basics (Module 13) is my addition. It is short, but I think it belongs in any induction.
- The practical check is five tasks and the facilitator records pass or not yet. Nothing automated.
- The refresher covers modules 3, 6, 7, 8 and 9 plus the standards ticks. Materials, money and calendar become a one-page handout for existing contractors.
- Call backs are explained on the big screen only. The demo does not plant a call back on the contractor's own copy; it felt wrong to start their account with one.
- Re-tick once a year, and after any material change to a module.

**Still open**

- [ ] **Afghan language.** Afghanistan's main languages are Dari and Pashto. Dari is very close to Farsi, so one translation may serve both Afghan and Iranian painters. Confirm Dari, Pashto or both.
- [ ] **Translation method.** Settled: machine translation first, your team tests each language and sends back changes, and a language goes live only when you say. Only the tick wording is translated; the demo and the app stay English.
- [ ] **Materials threshold.** "Short of something" is fine, but who pays if a contractor runs out because of waste? Not stated anywhere yet.
- [ ] **Practical check fails.** If someone cannot do the five tasks, do they repeat the app modules the same day, or come back?
- [ ] **Day-one visit.** Is the Project Coordinator able to do this for every new contractor, or only the first few while numbers are small?
