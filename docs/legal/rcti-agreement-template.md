### 1. What this agreement covers

This agreement applies to the following supplies made by the Contractor to
Paint Group ("the Supplies"):

- painting and related subcontract services performed under Paint Group work
  orders, including agreed variations to those work orders; and
- reimbursement of approved job expenses associated with those work orders.

### 2. The parties agree that

1. **Paint Group may issue recipient-created tax invoices** (RCTIs) in
   respect of the Supplies.
2. **The Contractor will not issue tax invoices** in respect of the Supplies
   while this agreement is in force.
3. The Contractor acknowledges that they are **registered for GST** with the
   ABN shown above, and will **notify Paint Group in writing immediately** if
   they cease to be registered for GST or if their registration details
   change. *(If the Contractor is not registered for GST, RCTIs cannot be
   issued and the Contractor's invoices are issued as ordinary invoices
   without GST — this agreement does not commence until registration is
   confirmed.)*
4. Paint Group acknowledges that it is registered for GST and will notify the
   Contractor in writing immediately if it ceases to be registered.
5. Paint Group will issue the original or a copy of each RCTI to the
   Contractor **within 28 days** of the supply being made or its value being
   determined, and will retain the original or a copy.
6. Paint Group will report and pay the GST shown on each RCTI to the extent
   required by law, and the Contractor will account for the GST on the
   Supplies in their activity statements as required by law.
7. Each RCTI will show the words **"Recipient-created tax invoice"**, both
   parties' identities and ABNs, the date, a description of the Supplies, and
   the GST-inclusive value and GST amount, as required for a tax invoice.
8. **Payment amounts** shown on each RCTI are derived from the accepted work
   order offer, customer-approved variations, agreed deductions, and approved
   expense reimbursements recorded on the Paint Group platform, which the
   Contractor can inspect at any time through their portal login. A dispute
   about any RCTI must be raised in writing within 7 days of issue.

### 3. Term and termination

This agreement takes effect on the date of the last signature below and
continues until either party ends it by written notice. It ends automatically
if either party ceases to be registered for GST, or if the ATO notifies
either party that RCTIs may no longer be issued under it.
