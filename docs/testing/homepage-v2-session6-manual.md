# Homepage v2 — session 6 (motion) · Tom's 90-second walkthrough

Open the homepage on your phone. Four things move now, and nothing else.

1. **The field types by itself.** About a second after the page loads, an
   address types itself into the address field, the matching chip lights
   ("My home" or "A business…"), and a line under the field shows the
   address → price range → how long it took. It holds, fades, and the next
   example starts. Four examples, then it loops. (⚑9.1: these are the
   brief's placeholder jobs until you supply real anonymised ones.)
2. **Touch it and it stops for good.** Tap the field, or a chip: the field
   is empty with the cursor in it, no ghost text anywhere, the chip is back
   on "My home". It does not start again while you're on the page.
3. **Watch it happen.** Scroll to "Watch it happen from wherever you are."
   When half the phone is on screen the story plays once: 22 seconds, eight
   captions on the left in step with the phone — message, photos, Day 3,
   the typed update, the $486 variation approving itself, Day 5, the
   walkthrough banner, the signature and the invoice. Then a small
   "↻ Replay" appears. Scrolling away and back does not replay it. Switch
   apps mid-story and come back: it resumes where it was.
4. **The live numbers count up** as the strip comes into view (38 · 6 ·
   100% · 9 min).

If your phone has "Reduce motion" on: the field sits empty and idle, the
story shows its last frame with the eight captions listed underneath, and
the numbers are just there.

Say the word if it's right — that approval is the brief's §7.6.
