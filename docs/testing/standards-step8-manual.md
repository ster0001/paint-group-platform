1. PC Command → **Contractors** (the tab replaces Painters). The strip: five colour counts, then Open call backs, Rewards due, Not signed. Add the five colours up — it equals the number of rows with a light.
2. Rows: Red first, Green last. Each shows the light in words, the trend arrow, Checks / App / Call backs / Streak as counts, and tags.
3. Tap a painter: their last 10 jobs as dots (tap one for why), the reason line, **Bonus history (staff only)** with amounts.
4. **Spot check** → pick the job under way → "Spot check added". The job page's Quality check card lists a spot check; the painter's job shows it.
5. **Log call back** → pick a finished job → you land on that job's Call backs card.
6. Home dashboard → Contractors section: **Painters on Green** equals the strip's Green count; **Rewards due** equals the strip; tap a tile for the rows.
7. Sign in as a painter: nothing of this is visible; their Home shows their own light only.
