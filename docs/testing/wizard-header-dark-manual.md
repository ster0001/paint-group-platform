# Manual test — the logo on a dark bar (20 Nov 2026)

What changed: on a phone the quote wizard's header (back · logo · Contact) is now a dark bar like the
website's, with the logo bigger (34px, was 22px) and exactly in the middle. The quote stage wears the
same bar on a phone and on a laptop (logo 42px there), and the laptop's question screens and the simple
pages (visit, sent, finish) have the dark bar too. No migration.

## A. Paint Group, on a phone
1. Open paintgroup.com.au on a phone and tap **See my price**.
2. The top of the screen is a dark bar. The logo is the same one the website's header shows, white
   on dark, in the middle, clearly bigger than before. **Contact** is a white outline pill on the right.
3. Answer a question: the **‹** back chevron appears on the left, in white. The logo does not move.
4. Carry on to the range, then **Get my quote range**: the same dark bar tops the quote stage.

## B. Paint Home, on a phone
1. Open painthome.com.au and do the same. The bar shows the Paint Home mark with white "PAINT"
   lettering. Nothing says Paint Group.

## C. On a laptop
1. Open a quote stage link (`/estimate/quote?id=…`) on a laptop: the dark bar runs the full width
   of the window with the logo in the middle.
2. Open **See my price** on a laptop: the header is the same dark bar — logo top-left (bigger, 34px),
   the steps along the middle with the one you are on in white, **Save & book** as a white outline pill.
3. The simple pages (booking a visit, "Thanks — your estimator has it") have the same dark bar with the logo.

## If the Paint Group logo shows as letters ("PAINT—GROUP") on the dark bar
Settings → Company has no dark-background logo (the first logo box — the one the website uses).
Upload it there; the light-background logo is never shown on the dark bar.
