## What this is for
Short films showing the portal being used, rather than described. Watch one before you use a screen for the first time. Each opens on YouTube in its own tab, so you keep your place here.

## Quick how-tos
One thing each. The first four are under a minute and made for a phone; the last is the long one, worth watching end to end when you first get your login.

1. [Company information](https://youtube.com/shorts/0dR0AVqccgw)
2. [Platform guide](https://youtube.com/shorts/c3STd0cC9KE)
3. [Accepting a job](https://youtube.com/shorts/CWnIApwbdiw)
4. [Preparing for the final walkthrough](https://youtube.com/shorts/EI8tkZdXDRQ)
5. [Updating your job in the system](https://youtu.be/IraRrURz-Hk)

## If something goes wrong
- **A link does nothing on a work phone.** The YouTube app may be blocked on that device. Open the link in the phone's browser instead.
- **A video is out of date against the screen.** The written guides in this Help centre are checked against the real screens and win. Ring the office and say which video.
