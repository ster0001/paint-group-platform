## Welcome to your Paint Group portal
target: /portal
This is where your jobs live: offers, bookings, the job sheet, your invoices. Six tabs at the bottom; this takes a minute.

## Home
target: /portal
Home tells you if anything needs your answer today — an offer, a variation, a quality check — and whether your insurance is current so Paint Group can offer you work.

## Requests
target: /portal/requests
Job offers land here with the dates, the calculated hours and your price, and a 24-hour clock. Accept, propose a different start date, or decline. Until you accept you see the suburb only.

## Jobs
target: /portal/jobs
Every booked job. Open one to tick surfaces off, take before and after photos, raise anything you find, and finish with the completion list. The customer signs off on your phone at the end.

## Invoicing
target: /portal/money
Send a progress claim whenever a job has money owing, check the invoice drafted for you at sign-off, and watch it go from with the office to paid.

## Calendar
target: /portal/calendar
Your booked days. Tap a free day to block it out; Paint Group sees it straight away and will not offer you those dates.

## Help
target: /portal/help
Every screen has a guide with pictures and a short film. Start here when you are stuck, and ring the office if the guide does not cover it. You can run this tour again from Help.
