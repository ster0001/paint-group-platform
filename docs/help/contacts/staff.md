## What this is for
**Contacts** in the sidebar is the office's address book: every person saved with **Save to Contacts** from an estimate's Contact card. The list is sorted by surname; the search box finds one without scrolling.

## Before you start
- A contact appears here only once it has been saved from an estimate. The list is empty on a fresh account and says so.
- You need the **Contacts** area on your login (Settings → Logins). A login without it lands on its first visible page instead.

## Steps
1. Open **Contacts**. The search box is at the top right of the list.
2. Type any part of a first name, surname, company, email, phone or suburb. The list narrows a moment after you stop typing; **Enter** runs it at once. A phone number matches with or without its spaces, so **0412345678** finds **0412 345 678**.
3. **Clear** (beside the box, once you have searched) brings the whole list back. The search is in the page address (`?q=`), so a bookmark or a back-button returns to the same result.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **No contact matches "…"** — nothing saved has those words. Check the spelling, or search a shorter part of it.
- **The contacts could not be loaded — this is not an empty list** (red) — the read itself failed; the list is not empty. Reload, and if it stays, tell the office.

## If something goes wrong
- A person you saved from an estimate is not here: open that estimate's Contact card and press **Save to Contacts** again — only a saved card makes a row.
