## What this is for
The level on your work order (Level 2, 3 or 4) tells you how much preparation to do and how fine the finish must be. The finish standards say exactly what that means, surface by surface. The office's quality check and the customer's walk-through are judged against the same words, so read the surface before you start it.

## Before you start
- You need to be signed in to the portal. The standards are under the **HELP** tab, so they are there even while your account is on hold.
- Your work order shows the job's level near the top, beside **Level of finish**. Tap the level chip for a one-screen summary of that level.
- The work order also says **Tape check required** or **Tape check not required**. On jobs under 16 hours the tape step in the defect rule is not required; you still check your own work.

## Steps

### Confirming the standards (once)
1. When Paint Group invites you, you get a text and an email with a link, and **Home** shows **Read and confirm the finish standards**. A new painter sees it as their first screen. Tap **Start**.
2. There are six short sections: the three levels and the look test, rules for every job, your time and variations, interior surfaces, exterior surfaces, the defect rule and final checklist. Read each one, tap **I have read and understand this**, then **Next section**. The sixth button reads **Confirm**. Nothing to type.
3. You can stop and come back: the next time you open the sign-off it starts at the first section you have not ticked.
4. When all six are confirmed you see **Standards confirmed** with the date and version. A copy is saved in your documents (**Profile → Insurance & licences** or **Your tickets**) and emailed to you as a PDF.
5. Until you confirm, Paint Group cannot send you new job offers once your grace period ends; jobs you have already started carry on as normal. The reminder texts stop the moment you confirm.

### From a job
1. Open **Jobs** and tap the job. Near the top is one closed drop-down, **What we expect on this job**, showing how many standards and lines it covers and the job's level.
2. Tap it once. It opens as one document: each standard appears once — for example **Walls · Level 3** — with the rooms it covers underneath (**Hallway · Walls**, **Bedroom · Walls** …), then **Every level**, then the rows for this job's level (Fill, Sand, Gaps, Lines and so on) and the look test distance. A line with no finish standard (gutters, a fuel allowance) is not listed. Tap the heading again to close it.
3. Tap **Open the full standard ›** under a standard to read that surface on its own page, locked to **Level 3 on this job**; **See other levels ›** compares; **← Job** comes back.

### From Help
1. Tap **HELP** in the bar at the bottom, then the **Finish standards** card at the top.
2. Choose **Interior** or **Exterior** and tap a surface. Use **Level 2 / Level 3 / Level 4** to switch levels. Exterior surfaces also carry **Rules for every exterior surface** at the bottom.
3. Under **The rules**, open **The three levels**, **Rules for every job**, **Your time and variations**, **The defect rule**, **Final checklist** or **Words we use**.

### Extra time and variations
1. **Your time and variations** lists the three extra-time items: **Bogging**, **Stain blocking** and **Additional coats**. If your work order allows time for one, it is on the job sheet.
2. If you find one of these and it is not on your work order, stop, take photos and tap **+ Found something** on the job. Pick **Bogging**, **Stain blocking** or **Additional coats** as the kind, say what you found, and send it. The office prices it and the customer approves before you do the work.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Blue pill "Level N on this job"** — the surface is locked to your job's level because you opened it from the work order.
- **Look test 1.5 m** (or 3 m, 0.5 m) — the distance you stand back at for that level.
- **Every level** — the part of the standard that applies whatever the level.

## If something goes wrong
- **"No job offers until you confirm the finish standards"** on Home — open the card and finish the six sections; offers resume at once.
- **"The finish standards could not be loaded"** — the office has not loaded them yet or the connection dropped. Pull down to reload; if it keeps happening, ring the office. The rest of the job page still works.
- **A surface is not in the What we expect card** — that line has no finish standard (for example gutters, downpipes, a deck). Ask the office if you are unsure what is expected.
- **The work order shows no level** — ask the office before you start.
