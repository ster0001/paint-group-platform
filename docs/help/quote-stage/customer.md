## What this is for
After **Your range today**, **Get my detailed price** takes you through the questions that turn your range into a detailed price, one at a time, on your phone. The price stays a range (repairs are confirmed on the day); it narrows as you answer.

## Before you start
You have your range and have given your name, mobile and email. Nothing you answer here commits you to anything.

## Steps
1. On your range, tap **Get my detailed price** (the big button, or the bar at the bottom). The thin bar at the top carries on from where the range left it.
2. **A few things first** — one question per screen, a tap each, and the next question comes up on its own: cornices (no, standard or decorative), how high the ceilings are, whether the doors are panelled or flat (with drawings), which type of windows (with drawings and a hint each), water-based or oil-based paint on the trims, what the woodwork was last painted in, whether the rooms will be cleared, a stairwell or void, where we can park, and a lift booking where the building needs one. **‹** goes back a question; your answer stays selected.
3. **How big is {room}?** — one screen per room. The grey numbers in Width, Length and Ceiling are typical for that room; type your own over them and tap **That's the size**, or tap **Not sure — use the typical size for now** to keep the typical (the room stays open and your range a little wider). No tape? Walk it out — one large step is about a metre.
4. **Your detailed price** — your range, which rooms stayed at the typical size, the three tiles, and **Book {estimator} to confirm it**, which takes you to the booking page.
5. **Contact** at the top right opens the sheet on every screen: Book a visit, and Call where the shopfront lists a number.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Grey numbers** in a size box — the typical size; yours replace them when you type.
- **Teal button** — the one door on the last screen.

## If something goes wrong
- A question will not save — the screen says so; check your connection and tap again.
- The link opens the older room-by-room editor — the detailed price stage is still switched off on that shopfront.
