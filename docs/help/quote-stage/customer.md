## What this is for
After **Your range today**, **Get my detailed price** takes you through the questions that turn your range into a detailed price, one at a time, on your phone. The price stays a range (repairs are confirmed on the day); it narrows as you answer.

## Before you start
You have your range and have given your name, mobile and email. Nothing you answer here commits you to anything.

## Steps
1. On your range, tap **Get my detailed price** (the big button, or the bar at the bottom). The thin bar at the top carries on from where the range left it.
2. **Your quote, above every question** — the **Quote** tile says how many lines are still to go and about how long that takes; tap it to open your draft quote (your name and address, a line per room with its size and what's included, the amount once that room is measured, repairs confirmed on the day, subtotal, GST and your range) and tap it again to close it. Under the tile, your range in large figures and **closing in · N% settled**; the thin bar beneath it shrinks inward and turns green as the rooms settle.
3. **A few things first** — one question per screen, a tap each, and the next question comes up on its own: cornices (no, standard or decorative), how high the ceilings are, whether the doors are panelled or flat (with drawings), which type of windows (with drawings and a hint each), water-based or oil-based paint on the trims, what the woodwork was last painted in, whether the rooms will be cleared, a stairwell or void, where we can park, and a lift booking where the building needs one. **‹** goes back a question; your answer stays selected.
4. **How big is {room}?** — one screen per room. The grey numbers in Width, Length and Ceiling are typical for that room; type your own over them and tap **That's the size**, or tap **Not sure — use the typical size for now** to keep the typical (the room stays open and your range a little wider). No tape? Walk it out — one large step is about a metre.
5. **Your detailed price** — your quote in full (now headed **Quote**, with **Your price** as a range — repairs are confirmed on the day), which rooms stayed at the typical size, the three tiles, and **Book {estimator} to confirm it**, which takes you to the booking page.
6. **Contact** at the top right opens the sheet on every screen: Book a visit, and Call where the shopfront lists a number.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Grey numbers** in a size box — the typical size; yours replace them when you type.
- **Teal button** — the one door on the last screen.
- **(typical)** beside a room's size — we used the usual size for a room like it; type its real size to replace it.
- **—** in the amount column — that room's figure comes once its size is settled.
- **Teal bars** on the Quote tile — lines that are settled; a mid-grey bar is a room left at its typical size.

## If something goes wrong
- A question will not save — the screen says so; check your connection and tap again.
- The link opens the older room-by-room editor — the detailed price stage is still switched off on that shopfront.
