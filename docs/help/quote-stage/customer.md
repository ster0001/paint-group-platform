## What this is for
After **Your range today**, **Get my detailed price** takes you through the questions that turn your range into a detailed price, one at a time, on your phone. The price stays a range (repairs are confirmed on the day); it narrows as you answer.

## Before you start
You have your range and have given your name, mobile and email. Nothing you answer here commits you to anything.

## Steps
1. On your range, tap **Get my detailed price** (the big button, or the bar at the bottom). The thin bar at the top carries on from where the range left it.
2. **Your room, drawn** — on every room card a small picture of that room sits beside the Quote tile and redraws with every answer: the doors and windows you count appear (and spill onto the side walls when the back wall is full), their size follows S / M / L, an unticked cornice or skirting disappears, walls go grey when you're not painting them, a robe appears on the right wall, and each repair you point out leaves its mark. Tap the picture to see it full width; × or Escape closes it.
3. **Your quote, above every question** — the **Quote** tile says how many lines are still to go and about how long that takes; tap it to open your draft quote (your name and address, a line per room with its size and what's included, the amount once that room is measured, repairs confirmed on the day, subtotal, GST and your range) and tap it again to close it. Under the tile, your range in large figures and **closing in · N% settled**; the thin bar beneath it shrinks inward and turns green as the rooms settle.
4. **A few things first** — one question per screen, a tap each, and the next question comes up on its own: cornices (no, standard or decorative), how high the ceilings are, whether the doors are panelled or flat (with drawings), which type of windows (with drawings and a hint each), water-based or oil-based paint on the trims, what the woodwork was last painted in, whether the rooms will be cleared, a stairwell or void, where we can park, and a lift booking where the building needs one. **‹** goes back a question; your answer stays selected.
5. **How big is {room}?** — the first of four cards for each room. The grey numbers in Width, Length and Ceiling are typical for that room; type your own over them and tap **That's the size**, or tap **Not sure — use the typical size for now** to keep the typical (the room stays open and your range a little wider). No tape? Walk it out — one large step is about a metre. **Add a photo of the room** is optional (up to three, 5 MB each).
6. **What we're painting in here** — the room's surfaces as chips; tap one to untick it. **Walls** carries All / 75% / 50% / 25%; **Doors** has − / + and door · + frame · + architrave; each window group has − / + and S / M / L (S under 50 × 50 cm, M to 1.5 m, L over 1.5 m). **+ Add a surface** lists what else we can paint, another window size, and a box for anything unusual (priced by your estimator). **Next**.
7. **Any built-in robes or cupboards being painted?** — only in rooms that have them. **No** moves on by itself; **Yes** asks about the doors and the walls inside, then **Next**.
8. **Anything needing attention?** — tap what you can see (crack, hole or dent, flaking, water mark, mould, wallpaper, feature wall, something else), say how much of it there is, and **Take a photo of the spot** if you can: with a photo we price the repair straight away, without one a person does. **Add it**, or **Nothing to fix — next**. Anything already noted shows above the chips with a × to remove it.
9. **Have we got every room?** — the list with each size. **+ Add a room** (pick its kind, name it, **Add it**) opens the new room's size card and its three other cards, then brings you back here. Answer the **doors and windows across the house — right?** check, then **That's every room**.
10. **Anything we haven't listed?** — priced extras from our rate card (tap one, − / + for how many), **Help me choose colours**, and a box for anything else, which a person prices. **Next**.
11. **{Your name}, your detailed price** — your quote in full (now headed **Quote**, with **Your price** as a range — repairs are confirmed on the day), which rooms stayed at the typical size (tap one in the quote to measure it now), the three tiles, and **Book {estimator} to confirm it**, which takes you to the booking page.
12. **Contact** at the top right opens the sheet on every screen: Book a visit, and Call where the shopfront lists a number.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Grey numbers** in a size box — the typical size; yours replace them when you type.
- **Teal button** — the one door on the last screen.
- **(typical)** beside a room's size — we used the usual size for a room like it; type its real size to replace it.
- **—** in the amount column — that room's figure comes once its size is settled.
- **Teal bars** on the Quote tile — lines that are settled; a mid-grey bar is a room left at its typical size.

## If something goes wrong
- A question will not save — the screen says so; check your connection and tap again.
- The link opens the older room-by-room editor — the detailed price stage is still switched off on that shopfront.
