## What this is for
Once your job is booked, **Your project, day by day** in your account is the record of the work: each update your painting team sends, the photos that go with it, where every area is up to, and who is on site.

## Before you start
- Sign in to your account with the emailed link (or the code in the same email).
- The page fills as the job happens. Before the first day it says **Nothing to show just yet**.

## Steps
1. Open your account and choose **My project**. With more than one job, the chips above the title switch between them (a card on Home opens its own job here).
2. **Where everything is up to** lists each area with **Not started**, **Being prepped**, **First coat** or **Done ✓**, and a **Day 3 of 6** chip while the job runs. Once you have approved an extra, the list carries one line for it — **Extra work · 1 approved · $250.00 inc GST** — instead of a Variations area.
3. **Who's at your home** names your painter by first name, and **Who is managing and overseeing the job** names your project coordinator with **Message me**. After you sign off, the same two cards read **Who painted your home** and **Who looked after your job**.
4. **Day by day** is the feed, newest first, and every day of the job keeps its photos — however long the job ran (since 27 Oct 2026; before that, only the newest three days or so showed theirs). Photos load as you scroll, so a card lower down may take a moment to fill in on a slow connection. A card headed **From your painting team** is an update the office checked and sent you (it says **Checked and sent by the office** underneath). The photos the office attached to that update sit on that card, whatever day they were taken (since 14 Oct 2026). Other site photos from that day sit under the same card; a day with photos but no update gets its own **Photos from the site** or **Before photos — how it started** card.
5. Each card with photos says how many (**4 photos · tap to view**). Tap one to see it full size with its room name and **2 of 4**; swipe, use the arrows or the ← → keys to move between the photos of that card; tap **Close** to come back.

## Updates from the office
Since 24 Oct 2026 the office sends short notes to your timeline as the job moves: when a whole surface is prepped or painted right through (*Walls are painted right through the house. Ceilings are up next.*) and a quick summary at the end of a working day. Part-way through a longer job you also get a short check-in asking how you are finding things, and on the morning the painters start marking up touch-ups a message saying so, by text and email as well as here (put a bit of tape on anything you want looked at). Each one carries who it is from — **Felipe, Paint Group** — under the words. There is one **Reply to Felipe** button for the whole timeline; it opens your message thread with the office, where every reply is read.

## What the labels mean
- **From your painting team** — an update sent to you; the same words came by email and text.
- **Before photos — how it started** — the record of each area before any paint went on.
- **Something needs your say-so** — a change to the job waiting for your approval; **Review & approve** opens it.
- **Quality check passed** — your coordinator checked the work against our standards.

## If something goes wrong
- **A photo card is empty.** Each photo loads as its card scrolls into view; on a slow connection give it a moment or scroll back up and down. If a card stays empty, use **Message me** and name the day — the office can check the photo is on your job.
- **An update's photos are not showing.** Updates sent before 14 Oct 2026 show only the photos taken on the day they were sent. Any photo the office attached since then shows on its update. If one is still missing, reply to the update email or use **Message me**; the office can resend it.
- **A photo looks like someone else's job.** Tell the office straight away; every photo is tied to your job and we will check it.
- **No email arrived for an update.** The update is still here on this page; check your junk folder and tell us so we can fix the address.
