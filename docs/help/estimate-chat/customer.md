## What this is for
Anything you want to check before deciding — a room, a colour, a date, the price — goes in the chat on your estimate. It is one conversation: the same thread shows in your account under Messages, and whoever answers at Paint Group sees exactly what you wrote.

## Before you start
Nothing. The chat works on the link we sent you, signed in or not.

## Steps
1. Open your estimate from the link in your text or email.
2. Press **Ask a question** at the top, **💬 Chat with us** in the bar at the bottom of the screen, or **Message us** under **Ready when you are**. The chat box opens in the bottom corner over the page.
3. Type your message and press **Send message**. It appears as your bubble.
4. Out of office hours you see a note: *Thanks — we've received your message. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30am to 4:30pm; we'll get back to you as soon as we're open.*
5. When we reply you get a text and an email with a link; opening it brings the chat back up with our answer. Press × to close the box any time; your messages stay.

## What the labels mean
- **You** — your messages, on the right.
- **Paint Group** (or the person's name) — our replies, on the left.
- **Amber note** — the office is closed right now; your message is safe and will be answered when we open.

## If something goes wrong
- **"That didn't go through."** Check your connection and press Send again; nothing is lost from the box.
- **No reply yet.** In office hours we aim to answer the same day. Outside them, the note tells you when we're back. You can always call the number on the estimate.
- **The box will not open.** On a small screen scroll a little so the bottom bar shows, then press **Chat with us**.
