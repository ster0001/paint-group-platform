## What this is for
After you answer the questions online and see your price range, you may want to look at it again, or ask about it, without starting over. The link in our texts and emails opens your own page: the range you were shown, the address, and a chat with the office.

## Before you start
Nothing. The page works from the link on any phone or computer, without signing in.

## Steps
1. Open the link in our text or email. The page says **Hi** with your first name, the address, and **Your range · incl. GST** with the range you were shown.
2. A job inside and outside shows both halves under the range: **Inside** and **Outside**.
3. To ask a question, type it in **Chat with us** at the bottom of the page and press **Send message**. It reaches the office straight away.
4. When we reply, it appears in the same chat, and we let you know by text and email. The link in that message brings you back here.
5. Prefer to talk? Press **Call** with our number.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Worked out … from your answers online** — the day the range was worked out. If you went back and changed an answer, the page shows the newest range you were shown.
- **This is a guide** — the range is not a fixed price. Your price is confirmed with you before anything is fixed.
- **We'll come and look** — on a bigger job we arrange a visit instead of pricing it from a phone.

## If something goes wrong
- **"Page not found"** — the link may be cut short in the message. Open it from the original text or email, or call us.
- **Out of office hours** the chat tells you we have your message and when we're back (Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm).
