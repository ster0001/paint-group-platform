## What this is for
Every invoice we send you — the deposit, a payment request during the job or the final balance — has one **Pay** button. Press it and everything you need for a bank transfer is in front of you.

## Before you start
- Open the invoice from the email we sent, or from **Invoicing** in your account with **View & pay**.

## Steps
1. Press **Pay $amount** under the invoice lines. The amount is what is due on this invoice now.
2. The box that opens shows the amount due (**Deposit due**, **Payment due** or **Final balance due**), then **Bank transfer**: our account name, bank, BSB and account number.
3. Use the invoice number shown as **Reference** when you make the transfer — press **Copy** to copy it. The reference is how we match your payment to your job straight away.
4. Make the transfer in your own banking app, then press **Done**. There is nothing to confirm on our page; we record the payment when it lands and email you a receipt.
5. If the box also offers **Or pay online by card**, that is a second choice. The card amount includes the surcharge shown on the button; paying the bank transfer amount avoids it.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Pay $amount** — the balance still owing on this invoice.
- **Paid in full — thank you** — the invoice is settled and a receipt has been issued.
- **Payment received — thank you** — shown after a card payment is confirmed.

## If something goes wrong
- **The box has no card option.** Card payments are not offered at the moment; bank transfer is the way to pay.
- **"Card payments aren't offered just now."** You followed an older card link. Press **Pay** on the invoice for the bank details.
- **No card payment was taken.** You came back from the card page without paying. The box opens again with the bank details; pay by transfer, or try the card again.
- Anything else: reply to the email the invoice arrived with or ring us, and we will sort it out together.
