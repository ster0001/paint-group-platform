## What this is for
Ask us anything before you start an estimate, or while you are filling one in. Someone from the office answers in the same box.

## Before you start
Nothing. The bubble is in the bottom corner of the homepage, every website page and the online estimate.

## Steps
1. Tap **Chat with us** in the bottom corner. The chat opens with a hello.
2. Type your question and press **Send**.
3. If we don't have your details yet, the chat asks for your name, email and mobile, and shows three boxes under the question. Fill them in and press **Send my details**. You can also type them into a message instead, for example *jo@example.com 0412 345 678*.
4. The chat says *Thanks — we've got your details and the team will come back to you.* The boxes go away and it doesn't ask again.
5. Out of office hours the chat says when someone is next available, and asks for your details in the same message. We ring or email you when we open.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **We answer here**: nobody has picked the chat up yet.
- **Finding someone…**: your message is with the office and waiting for a person.
- **A person is with you**: someone from the office is answering now.

## If something goes wrong
- **"That number doesn't look right"**: enter an Australian number, for example 0412 345 678.
- **"Chat isn't available just now"**: wait a moment and tap the bubble again, or ring us on the number at the top of the page.
