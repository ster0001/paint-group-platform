## What this is for
Paint Group sends you a private link to join the portal. The link opens a short form that creates your login. Afterwards, **My profile** is where your details live — including the mobile every text from us goes to.

## Before you start
- The email the invitation was sent to. Your login is that address and it cannot be changed on the form.
- Your mobile. It is required: job offers, approved changes to a job, quality-check notes and the reminders to update your work order all come to it by text.

## Steps
1. Open the link from the invitation email.
2. Check your name, then enter **Your mobile** as a full Australian mobile, 04xx xxx xxx.
3. Choose a password of at least 8 characters and type it again.
4. Press **Create my account**. You land in your portal with the mobile already on your record.
5. Next, on **My profile**, add your ABN, bank details and insurance certificate — Paint Group cannot offer you work until the insurance is on file.

### Changing your mobile later
6. Open **My profile** and find the **Your mobile** card. Type the new number and press **Save mobile**. It must be a full mobile; the field cannot be left empty.
7. If your profile has no mobile on it (painters who joined before this was required), the page says **Your mobile is required** at the top, and the company details and bank cards will not save until you add it.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Your mobile — required** on the card, and the red notice at the top — there is no mobile on your record yet; nothing else on the page saves until there is.
- **Saved.** — the number is on your record and the next text comes to it.

## If something goes wrong
- **"Your mobile is required."** Enter it before pressing Create my account.
- **"That doesn't look like a full Australian mobile."** All ten digits, 04xx xxx xxx. Landlines do not take texts and are refused.
- **"Add your mobile first — it's required."** You pressed Save on another card with no mobile on file. Save the mobile, then save the card again.
- **You joined but your mobile is missing on My profile.** Rare — the number did not save at the moment you joined. Add it on the profile page; nothing else is affected.
- Locked out afterwards: logins/contractor.md.
