## What this is for
Employed painters who lead jobs get the same traffic light as contractors, scored on the jobs they led: quality checks passed first time, reminder texts answered, and call backs, on the last 10 signed-off jobs. Green makes you bonus eligible — a review every 4 clean jobs of 16 hours or more. Priority on new jobs and faster payment are contractor things and never appear on your screens. Nothing is ever taken off you.

## Before you start
You see a status only once you have led a job that has been signed off. A crew member on someone else's job has no status card and no My status page — that is correct, not a fault.

## Steps
1. Open **Home**. The **Your status · jobs you led** card shows the light, the word and the bars to Green. Tap it.
2. **My status** shows why you are on that colour, your steps to Green or your streak, **What you get** (a bonus review on Green; on Orange a quality check on every job you lead; on Red, talk with Paint Group before you lead another job), the three measures as counts, your last 10 led jobs as dots, three tips and the colour key.
3. Tap a dot to see the job and what went wrong.

## What the colours and labels mean
Same rules as contractors: Green = last 4 jobs clean; Yellow = 8 in 10 or better and no more than 1 call back; Orange = 5 to 8 in 10 or 2 to 3 call backs; Red = under 5 in 10 or 4 or more call backs; New = your first 4 led jobs.

## If something goes wrong
- **No card** — you have not led a signed-off job yet, or the office has status switched off for painters.
- **A job you worked on is missing** — only jobs you LED count for you.
