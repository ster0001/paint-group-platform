## What this is for
Your status is a traffic light: **Green**, **Yellow**, **Orange** or **Red**, and **New** for your first jobs. It comes from three things on your last 10 signed-off jobs: quality checks passed first time, reminder texts answered the same day, and call backs. A job counts 7 days after the customer signs it off. Green gets you priority on new jobs, payment within 3 business days of sign-off, and a bonus review every 4 clean jobs of 16 hours or more. Nothing is ever taken off you. Customers never see it.

## Before you start
Paint Group switches status on for painters. Until then there is no card and no My status page — nothing is wrong with your account.

## Steps
1. Open **Home**. The **Your status** card shows the light with one lamp lit (or a blue ring for New), the status word, one line on what it gets you, and the bars to Green when you are not Green. Tap the card.
2. **My status** opens. At the top: the light, the word, and one line on why — for example "One call back in your last 10 jobs."
3. **Steps to Green** (or **Clean jobs in a row** when Green): how many clean jobs in a row you have, and how many more you need. Green also shows **Next bonus review**, a count such as "3 of 4". There is never an amount on your screen; Paint Group sets it each time.
4. **What you get on …**: ticks for what your colour gives you, dots for what unlocks on Green, exclamation marks for what changes on Orange or Red.
5. **Your last 10 jobs**: the three measures as counts ("5 of 5 passed first time", "28 of 30 reminders answered", "None"), then a dot per job — a tick is a clean job, an exclamation mark is not. Oldest on the left. Tap a dot to see the job, its hours and what went wrong. A job under 16 hours counts for your colour but not for a bonus. New shows the slots still to fill.
6. **What to do next**: three tips written from the measure that is holding you back.
7. **How the colours work**: tap to open. The rule and what you get for each colour.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Green** — your last 4 jobs were all clean. Priority on new jobs, paid within 3 business days of sign-off, bonus eligible.
- **Yellow** — 8 in 10 or better on checks and reminders, no more than 1 call back. Normal offers and payment; a quality check on 1 job in 3.
- **Orange** — between 5 and 8 in 10, or 2 to 3 call backs. Offers come after Green and Yellow; a quality check on every job; Paint Group will call you.
- **Red** — under 5 in 10, or 4 or more call backs. No new offers until you have talked with Paint Group. Jobs you have started continue.
- **New** — your first 4 jobs. Normal offers and payment; a quality check on your first jobs.
- The lit lamp pulses slowly. If your phone is set to reduce motion, it does not.

## If something goes wrong
- **No status card on Home** — Paint Group has not switched status on yet, or your account is a crew login (status belongs to the account that accepts offers). Ask the office.
- **A job is missing from the dots** — it is still inside its 7 days, or it was signed off before the status launch date and is never counted.
- **A reason looks wrong** — a call back that was voided or changed is recomputed within the half hour; ask the office if it is still there the next day.
