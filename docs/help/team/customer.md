## What this is for
If your organisation (an agency, builder or property manager) has a Paint Group workspace, its admins decide who else can see it. Each person gets a seat with an access level: **Admin**, **Approver**, **Viewer** or **Finance**. Every seat sees the whole organisation.

## Before you start
- You must be an admin (or the owner) of the organisation. Other people don't see the **Invite someone** card.
- You need the person's email address. It doesn't matter whether they have signed in to Paint Group before — for example as a customer on their own home. Their existing login is reused and gets this organisation added to it; nothing they already had changes.

## Steps
1. Open **Team** in your workspace.
2. In **Invite someone**, type their **Email**.
3. Choose **Their access**:
   - **Admin** — everything, including the Team screen.
   - **Approver** — approves estimates and variations.
   - **Viewer** — sees progress, approves nothing.
   - **Finance** — invoices and statements only.
4. For an Admin or Approver, you can set an **Approval limit, $**. Leave it blank for no limit; going over the limit only shows a warning.
5. Press **Send the invite**. The card shows **Invited ✓ — they've been emailed a sign-in link.** and they appear under **People**.
6. To change how someone hears about things, press **Updates** beside their name.

Inviting someone who is already in your organisation updates their access level and limit instead of adding them twice.

## If something goes wrong
- **"Couldn't set up that login just now — try again in a minute."** Something failed on our side; the office has been told automatically. Try again in a minute, and if it happens again contact the office.
- **"Only your organisation's admin can invite people."** Your seat isn't an admin seat. Ask your organisation's admin to invite them, or to make you an admin.
- **They didn't get the email.** Check the address on the **People** list, and ask them to look in junk mail. Inviting them again sends a fresh link.
