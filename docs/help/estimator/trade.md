## What this is for
A trade account is for someone who quotes painting forty times a year, not once: a property manager, a strata manager, a builder. The same estimate, composed for a desk — denser screens, your saved specs, and everything your estimator has already measured carried forward.

## Before you start
You need a trade account (the office sets it up) and a sign-in link — there is no password. Your properties are listed on your account home; the office can add more.

## Steps
1. **Home.** "Waiting on us" is first: quotes with your estimator, and quotes confirmed and ready to accept. Then **Quote again from the file** for any property your estimator has measured, then your portfolio.
2. **+ New quote.** Pick the property. A measured property shows its file: last painted, areas measured, colours on the register, access notes. The line "everything here carries into the quote" means exactly that — you will only be asked what has changed.
3. Pick a **spec** (End-of-lease repaint, Vacate touch-up…) or **Start blank**. With a measured property and a spec, the range shows before you open the sheet.
4. **Open the sheet.** Every area down the side, walls / ceiling / trims / doors / windows across. Tap a coat cell to cycle **1c → 2c → not painted**; doors and windows count up, then off. The range in the panel moves as you go. On a phone the grid scrolls sideways.
5. **Colours** come from the property's register — the colours actually painted last time. Say in the confirmation if they are changing.
6. **Ask the tenant for photos.** From the sheet, enter the tenant's mobile and tick what you want (each room from the doorway, anything damaged…). They get a text with a link, no account or app; their photos land on this property and your estimator sees them. The message says who we are and that it has nothing to do with their bond — you can read it before it goes.
7. **Send for confirmation.** Every trade quote goes to your estimator — there is no fix-it-yourself on a trade account. Usually confirmed within a day, and usually without a visit. It then appears under "Waiting on us" as ready to accept.
8. **Save a spec** from any finished quote (New estimate → Save as a spec) and it is one tap next time.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Measured · rebook** — this property has a file; quotes start from it.
- **With <name>** — your estimator has it. **Ready to accept** — confirmed; the price is held for the days shown.
- **Guide / Detailed / Confirmed** under the range — the same tier bar the home estimate uses.

## If something goes wrong
- "This spec paints nothing that is on the file" — the spec's surfaces are not on the measured rooms; open the sheet and tick what you need.
- A quote under our minimum is refused online — ring the office; small jobs are priced from the call-out.
- The tenant did not get the text — open the tenant screen again; the link is shown there to copy and send yourself.
