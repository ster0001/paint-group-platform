## What this is for
Commercial painting is not one thing. A few offices can be priced from a desk; a strata building or a hospital needs someone to see it first. The online estimate sorts that out on the first commercial screen so you are never asked questions that do not apply, and never shown a number we would not stand behind.

## Before you start
Know which kind of place it is and roughly its size. For a warehouse, the floor area bracket and the wall height. For anything with a meeting date (a strata committee), have the date.

## Steps
1. Screen 1: the address, and inside / outside / both. Screen 2: choose **Commercial building**.
2. **What kind of place?** Eight tiles. Each says either **online · or we visit** or **we visit**. Tap one. The row underneath asks which part — inside, outside or both. Outside or both on any commercial job means a visit: heights and access cannot be read from a form.
3. **A range tile** (office, warehouse, retail, aged care and clinics, education inside): two screens. The areas — how many offices, open-plan areas, meeting rooms, or for a warehouse the area bracket, height, wall material, racking and access — then the job: surfaces, colours, condition in your building's own words, hours (business, after hours, closed) and whether it stays occupied.
4. **See my guide range.** The range is deliberately a little wider for commercial work; the note under it says by how much and why. **No price is fixed online for a commercial job** — your estimator confirms it, usually without a visit, and you accept from the confirmed number.
5. **A brief tile** (strata, hospitals, the education exterior) or any outside work: a short brief — what the job is, a date if one matters, a few photos, anything we should know — and the booking screen with four real times. Nothing is priced; the visit is quick because the brief comes with us.
6. Sending: from the range, **Tighten it online** or **Send to <your estimator>**. From a brief, **Book it**. Either way you get an email with a sign-in link to the estimate.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **ONLINE · OR WE VISIT** on a tile — a range now; a person confirms it.
- **WE VISIT** on a tile — a brief and a booking; no number until the visit.
- **Amber flags** on the range — things widening it (no photos yet, an exposed ceiling, after-hours work). Each says what would tighten it.

## If something goes wrong
- The tile you need is not there — pick the closest, or tap **Book a visit** and say what it is; a person sorts it.
- The range vanished after a change — an answer moved the job into visit territory (a large space, outside work, a hospital). The screen says which.
- No booking times suit — choose **Call me back** on the booking screen; the office rings within one working day.
