## What this is for
The online estimate is for anyone who wants a painting price without waiting for a visit. You answer a handful of questions, see a guide range straight away, and then decide how far to take it: leave it there, tighten it yourself, or hand it to a person. Nothing is booked and nothing is owed until you say so.

## Before you start
Have the address handy and know roughly what you want painted — inside, outside or both. A floorplan or a few photos help, but you do not need them to get a range.

## Steps
1. Open **Get your estimate** from the homepage. The first screen asks the address and **What's being painted?** — Inside, Outside or Both. The steps you will go through are listed along the top, with the one you are on highlighted.
2. On a laptop the questions sit on the left and a picture of your job on the right: the house changes with the kind of place and the storeys, the room paints itself in as you tick surfaces, and the floor plan fills in as you add or leave out rooms. Under the picture, **Your job so far** lists every answer with a **Change** link. On a phone the picture sits above the question.
3. **Inside:** the place (house, townhouse, unit; bedrooms and storeys; an optional floorplan), then what is being painted and what is changing colour, then the rooms, then the condition. Under **Any of them going much lighter, or a bold colour?**, tick **Which ones?** and those surfaces paint **deep blue** in the room picture — that is the undercoat and third coat, and it is the only time you are asked about it.
   - **A floorplan.** Upload it on the place screen. While it is being read, the picture on the right shows "Finding the rooms on the plan" ticking through; tap **Don't wait** to carry on from your answers — the plan's rooms swap in when the read finishes. Once read, the picture is your own plan; pinch or use **+** / **−** to zoom. Rooms you add or take out change the list, not the plan image. A carport, a sauna and anything labelled store or storage are left out of the price.
4. **Outside:** first **What kind of home is it?** — nine shape cards, each with its own small silhouette: weatherboard home, Californian bungalow, brick veneer, double storey, townhouse, unit or villa, period terrace, modern home, something else. Tap one and the picture draws the house from the street; **About how big is it?** scales it (the caption says the typical width and depth); a townhouse, unit or terrace also asks **Where is it in the row?** and draws a dashed "next door" on each shared side. You need a shape and a size to continue. Then **Tap everything we're painting** — the parts of the house in four groups (the walls and gable ends; fascias, gutters, downpipes, eaves; windows, doors, garage door, shutters; the verandah, and once it is ticked its posts, ceiling and handrail) and the things around the house (fences, deck, pergola, shed, boundary wall, side gate, meter box, security door). Each part lights up on the drawn house as you tick it, and tapping a part on the picture ticks it too; nothing is ticked for you, and nothing about materials is asked here. Then **Tell us about what you ticked** — only for the ticked parts: what the walls are made of (one material on a single storey; downstairs and upstairs on a double storey, with **Same as downstairs** first), whether brick has been painted before, what fills the gable, the window type (the drawing changes), single or double garage, the kind of posts, the rough sizes of the extras (a pergola is priced on its top's length × width), then the colours and anything tricky about access. The picture's texture follows every answer. Then **Check each side of the house** — four tiles (Front, Right, Back, Left) with the picture turned to the side you are on and a compass in its corner. Each side starts with what you ticked for the house: untick a part that isn't on that side, **+ Add something on this side** for one that is (a gable on the side, a porch at the back), pick a different material for that side's walls if it differs, or **Not painting this side** (a townhouse, unit or terrace starts its shared sides that way; tap again to add one back). Before that, **Which sides are we painting?** on About it ticks the sides (a shared wall starts unticked); only the ticked sides have a tile here. Under the parts, **Count them** has − and + for the windows and doors on that side, and the picture redraws with the count. There is nothing to confirm on each side, but you have to look at every ticked side (its tile ticks) before **Continue**. A gable ticked on a hip-roofed home lands on the front as a street-facing gable; add it to any other side from the side's list. A side left not painting is not on the estimate at all. Sizes come after your range.
4a. **Would you rather talk it through?** is a slim bar in the bottom-right corner of every wizard screen and stays there as you scroll; on a phone it is a bar of the three options just above the Continue button. Request a site visit, call, or send a message from any step.
5. **Both:** choose **price them yourself, one after the other**, or book an estimator for both. Inside comes first, then outside.
6. The last question asks where to send your estimate (name, email and mobile). Then **See my guide range**. The range card shows the range, how it tightens (Guide, then Detailed, then Confirmed — or a visit for outside work), and the choices: **Tighten my price**, **Book a site visit**, **Send us a message**. On a laptop a **Tighten my price** button also sits at the foot of the range card; on a phone it stays along the bottom of the screen. A "both" job shows the inside and outside ranges under the total; they add up to it. An outside job on the drawn house also lists one row per side — its size and what was counted on it — and one for around the house, then what is not in the number (scaffolding or a lift, rotten timber).
7. **Tighten my price** opens the editor on every path, the drawn house included (the walk-around step is gone). On an outside job the editor starts with **A few questions first**, which glows until it is answered: how the paintwork is holding up (each answer shows what it looks like), any timber rot, and anything tricky about access — all three are asked. Then each side asks its size; typing a new width also sets the run of that side's fascias, gutters and eaves. Under a side's windows the sizes are spelled out (small under 50 × 50 cm, medium 50 cm to 1.5 m, large over 1.5 m) and **+ Add a different sized window** adds a group. With the questions answered and every side confirmed the range closes to within a few percent. The older walk-around text that followed here described the retired step: right (**Looks right**, **Adjust it** in half-metre steps, or **Not sure** — it keeps the typical and is checked on the visit), and how the paint is holding up (good, weathered, peeling). **Check this side** waits for the size answer, turns the tile and moves to the next side; when every side is checked, **See my range** re-prices your estimate from what you counted and the range reads **Detailed**, each side's row marked checked. The first door then reads **Change a side**; **Book a site visit** is the main one, and **Change anything else** opens the side-by-side editor below with your sizes and the paintwork already filled in. On a laptop your cards are on the left and a panel on the right holds **Your range** (the range, how far it has narrowed, a checklist and the two buttons), **Your home** (your plan, or a drawn one) and your estimator. On a phone the range sits in a dark strip under the header and the two buttons along the bottom.
   - **Inside, room by room.** One card is open at a time, outlined in amber. Each asks its size first — the length and width are written in the question as boxes: tap **Looks right**, or tap a number, retype it (and the ceiling height if this room's differs) and tap **Update size**. Then what we are painting in there (Walls first), then cupboards and robes. Every cupboard question starts as **No** — robe doors included — and **Paint the walls inside the built-in robe?** (or inside the kitchen cupboards, the vanity, the linen cupboard) is its own question, so a mirrored robe can keep its doors and still have the inside painted. Tap a room's **name** to rename it. **+ Anything needing extra prep in here?** opens a short panel: attach photos of areas that need attention, tap what it is (**Something else** opens a box for your own words), say how much of it there is, then **Add it**. Under the rooms, **Anything we haven't listed** prices the card's extras (an air vent is $50 each; a ticked one shows − / + for how many). **Confirm** the room and the next one opens; a checked room shows a tick and a one-line summary.
   - **Outside, side by side.** A few questions about the paintwork, timber rot and access come first, one at a time. Then each side: **Are we painting this side?** (**Yes**, or **No, leave it off** — the card goes dashed and reads "Not painting"), its size, the walls on that side (with two materials, give each a share; they must add up to 100%), and anything else on that side. **Your home from above** shows each side amber until checked and cyan once it is; tap a side to open it.
   - **Both, on one page.** The rooms first, then "Now the outside, one side at a time". The **Inside** and **Outside** buttons at the top jump between the two halves. Checking the last room opens the front straight away.
8. When you are ready, **Finalise my price**. The Finalise page shows your detailed range and what you have told us on the left, and **Make it a fixed price** on the right: **Fix my price online** when the job qualifies, otherwise **Send for confirmation** (your estimator checks it and fixes the price) — and **Book a site visit instead** either way.
9. After you send it you see **Thanks — your estimator has it**, with what happens next and visit times if you would still like one (**Prefer a visit anyway?**). When you asked for a visit, the status line reads **Thanks — your estimator will contact you**: they go through all of the information with you and, if required, organise a site inspection. We email you a link to the estimate; open it from any device, no password needed.
10. **Talk it through**, **Send us a message** and **Save & book** open as a panel from the right on a laptop, or a sheet from the bottom on a phone. Close it with the × in the corner; nothing you have answered is lost.

## On a phone
On a phone (any screen narrower than about 770px) the same questions come one at a time, each on its own screen, so a thumb can answer them. Nothing you answer on a phone is different from a laptop — only the way it is laid out.

1. The top of every screen is the same: a **‹** back chevron on the left (it retraces your screens one at a time; it is hidden on the first), the logo in the middle, and **Contact** on the right. Under them a thin bar fills as you go, and a strip with a small picture of your job beside a line of your answers so far ("Inside · House · 3 bed · Two storeys …"). The picture is the room for an inside job and the house for an outside one; it follows your answers — a unit or apartment is a block, a townhouse a pair, two storeys adds a window row, the colours tint the walls and the condition marks them. Tap the strip to see it full width with the same surface chips.
2. **What are we painting?** — your address sits in a chip with **Change** (tap it to type a different address; if the address came through as words only, the suburb and postcode boxes appear here). Tap **Inside** or **Outside**.
3. **What kind of place is it?** — House, Townhouse, Unit or apartment. A single-choice screen like this moves on by itself a moment after you tap; there is no Continue button to find.
4. **How many bedrooms?** — 1 to 5+. This builds your room list for you.
5. **One storey or two?** — skipped for a unit or apartment.
6. **What's the job?** — The whole interior, Some rooms, or Doors, windows and trims.
7. **What's being painted?** — the surfaces as chips, pre-ticked from the job you chose (Doors, windows and trims pre-ticks skirtings, doors and window frames only). Each tick rolls that surface's paint on in the little picture (and off again when you untick); the strip's second line counts the surfaces ticked. Untick anything we are not painting, then **Continue**.
8. **We're painting these rooms** (or **Which rooms?** after Some rooms, which pre-ticks the first two) — the rooms we have guessed from your bedrooms and storeys, each a chip you can untick; Study, Ensuite and Garage are offered unticked; **+ Another room** adds one by name. **Continue** waits until at least one room is ticked.
9. **Colours?** — New colours, Same colours, or Still deciding (priced as new colours).
10. **How are the walls looking?** — Good, just tired · Some wear · Needs work.
11. **Anyone living there while we paint?** — No, it'll be empty · Yes, we'll be there.
12. **Where shall we send your range?** — your name, mobile and email, and an optional offers tick. **Show my range** lights up once the three are in; your range appears instantly on the next screen, exactly as it does on a laptop.

- **Outside** on a phone asks the place, then the outside questions as they are (what kind of home, what we are painting, about it, each side) with a **Continue** bar under each — the drawn house sits above the question, and the strip's small house paints in the parts you tick (walls, eaves and fascia, gutters and downpipes, window frames, doors, verandah); the strip's chips tick those parts too.
- **Back** keeps every answer, and so does closing the page: come back on the same phone and **Welcome back** lands you on the screen you left, with your answers in place. **Start again** clears them.
- **Contact** opens the same options as the laptop's talk-it-through bar: request a call (or a site visit on the Paint Group site), ring us, or send a message.

## The estimate page, once it is sent
- **Photos and videos.** "Your property, as we saw it" shows the first nine photos — and any video you sent us, which plays right there on the page — from your enquiry and our site notes. When there are more, a **More photos (N)** button sits underneath; press it and the rest open below (the button then reads **Fewer photos**).
- **Counts.** In "Scope of works, item by item", anything we price per item — doors, windows, posts, architraves — shows how many we counted in that room, e.g. **Doors × 4**. A single item shows no number, and walls and ceilings are measured, not counted, so they carry none either. If a count looks wrong, tell your estimator before accepting; the price follows the count.

## The PDF, and the colour note
- **Download estimate (PDF)** prints a paper copy of the quote: every area and its price (per-item surfaces with their count, e.g. "Doors × 4"), the deposit, the paint being supplied, what is and is not included, and a **Payment details** box with Paint Group's ABN, bank account and the reference to use — so the deposit can be paid straight from the PDF.
- **SWMS.** When Paint Group has attached a Safe Work Method Statement for your job, a **Download SWMS** button sits on the SWMS card beside the public liability card (or on its own **SWMS** card under "Why Melburnians choose us"). It opens as a PDF.
- **Colour consultation included** shows near the top while any colour on the job is still to be decided. Once every colour has been chosen it disappears — there is nothing left to consult on.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Amber** — still to check, or something we assumed (a typical size, an answer we filled in "from what you told us"). Tap to confirm or change it.
- **Cyan** — checked by you.
- **Grey, dashed** — left off: a room or a side we are not painting.
- **Guide / Detailed / Confirmed** — how tight the range is; it narrows as you check rooms and sides.

## If something goes wrong
- "Online estimates are nearly here" — the online estimate is switched off at the moment. Leave your number and we will call you.
- The address will not look up — type the suburb and postcode in the two boxes that appear; that is all the price needs.
- A screen says we would like someone to look at this first (outside the areas we cover, a very small job, or nothing we could price) — tap **Request a call back**, or ring the number shown. Everything you entered is kept.
- Anything else: tap the chat button at the top, **Talk it through**, or ring the number at the top of every screen.
