## What this is for
The online estimate is for anyone who wants a painting price without waiting for a visit. You answer a handful of questions, see a guide range straight away, and then decide how far to take it: leave it there, tighten it yourself, or hand it to a person. Nothing is booked and nothing is owed until you say so.

## Before you start
Have the address handy and know roughly what you want painted (inside, outside or both). A floorplan or a few photos help but are not needed to get a range.

## Steps
1. Open **Get your estimate** from the homepage. Screen 1 asks the address and whether it is inside, outside or both. **Book someone in** is there from the first screen if you would rather talk.
2. Screen 2: the place — house, townhouse, unit or a commercial building; bedrooms and storeys. Screen 3: what is painted and what is changing colour. Screen 4: the condition and whether anyone is living there.
3. **Outside jobs.** The outside screen is picture tiles — tick what we're painting on the house and what stands on its own; the questions that follow only cover what you ticked. The next screen asks **which sides** we're painting, looking from the street; a side you untick is left off the estimate altogether, and there is no way to add it back. On the tighten screen the × on a side's header deletes it the same way, like a room inside. The paintwork's condition is not asked here — the range is priced from good to peeling, and the tighten screen asks it first.
4. **See my guide range.** The range is priced from typical room sizes for a home like yours, and the screen says exactly what it assumed. The tier bar shows how tight the range is (Guide, Detailed, Confirmed).
5. Prefer to describe it? Tap the **chat bubble** in the corner, type a sentence or two about the job, and tap **Fill it in**. Anything we fill in from your words is tagged amber ("from what you told us") until you confirm it — tapping the answer, or Continue on that screen, confirms it.
6. From the range, **Tighten it online** opens the room-by-room view (inside) or the side-by-side view (outside). Outside, a few questions come first, one at a time: how the paintwork is holding up (if it's peeling, which sides — with a box for a photo), any timber rot anywhere (and where), and access. Then each side asks its size; the last two checks — the window and door counts, and anything missed — each confirm with a tick box. Each room asks one thing first: is this size about right? "Looks right" or "Adjust it". Then the surfaces, doors and windows, and anything needing attention. The range moves as you go.
7. Add photos wherever the screen offers — a photo of a damaged spot lets your estimator price it without coming out.
8. When you are done, the footer says what happens next. If the job qualifies, **Fix my price online** gives you one number, held for the days shown. Otherwise **Send to <your estimator>** and they confirm it, usually by the next working day. **Book a visit** is always there too.
9. Keep it: enter your email at the bottom of the range and we send a sign-in link. You can come back to the same estimate from any device — no password, ever.

## The estimate page, once it is sent
- **Photos.** "Your property, as we saw it" shows the first nine photos from your enquiry and our site notes. When there are more, a **More photos (N)** button sits underneath; press it and the rest open below (the button then reads **Fewer photos**).
- **Counts.** In "Scope of works, item by item", anything we price per item — doors, windows, posts, architraves — shows how many we counted in that room, e.g. **Doors × 4**. Walls and ceilings are measured, not counted, so they carry no number. If a count looks wrong, tell your estimator before accepting; the price follows the count.

## The PDF, and the colour note
- **Download estimate (PDF)** prints a paper copy of the quote: every area and its price (per-item surfaces with their count, e.g. "Doors × 4"), the deposit, the paint being supplied, what is and is not included, and a **Payment details** box with Paint Group's ABN, bank account and the reference to use — so the deposit can be paid straight from the PDF.
- **SWMS.** When Paint Group has attached a Safe Work Method Statement for your job, a **Download SWMS** button sits on the SWMS card beside the public liability card (or on its own **SWMS** card under "Why Melburnians choose us"). It opens as a PDF.
- **Colour consultation included** shows near the top while any colour on the job is still to be decided. Once every colour has been chosen it disappears — there is nothing left to consult on.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Amber** — assumed. A typical size, a surface we guessed, an answer the assistant filled in. Tap to confirm or change.
- **Cyan** — confirmed by you.
- **Guide / Detailed / Confirmed** — how tight the range is; it tightens as you confirm rooms.

## If something goes wrong
- "Online estimates are nearly here" — the online estimate is switched off at the moment. Leave your number and we call you.
- The address will not look up — type the suburb and postcode in the two fields that appear; that is all the price needs.
- The range disappears after an edit — some answers need a person (a large job, outside work, damage we cannot see). The screen says why and offers a visit or a call.
- Anything else: ring the number at the top of every screen.
