## What this is for
The online estimate is for anyone who wants a painting price without waiting for a visit. You answer a handful of questions, see a guide range straight away, and then decide how far to take it: leave it there, tighten it yourself, or hand it to a person. Nothing is booked and nothing is owed until you say so.

## Before you start
Have the address handy and know roughly what you want painted (inside, outside or both). A floorplan or a few photos help but are not needed to get a range.

## Steps
1. Open **Get your estimate** from the homepage. Screen 1 asks the address and whether it is inside, outside or both. **Book someone in** is there from the first screen if you would rather talk.
2. Screen 2: the place — house, townhouse, unit or a commercial building; bedrooms and storeys. Screen 3: what is painted and what is changing colour. Screen 4: the condition and whether anyone is living there.
3. **See my guide range.** The range is priced from typical room sizes for a home like yours, and the screen says exactly what it assumed. The tier bar shows how tight the range is (Guide, Detailed, Confirmed).
4. Prefer to describe it? Tap the **chat bubble** in the corner, type a sentence or two about the job, and tap **Fill it in**. Anything we fill in from your words is tagged amber ("from what you told us") until you confirm it — tapping the answer, or Continue on that screen, confirms it.
5. From the range, **Tighten it online** opens the room-by-room view. Each room asks one thing first: is this size about right? "Looks right" or "Adjust it". Then the surfaces, doors and windows, and anything needing attention. The range moves as you go.
6. Add photos wherever the screen offers — a photo of a damaged spot lets your estimator price it without coming out.
7. When you are done, the footer says what happens next. If the job qualifies, **Fix my price online** gives you one number, held for the days shown. Otherwise **Send to <your estimator>** and they confirm it, usually by the next working day. **Book a visit** is always there too.
8. Keep it: enter your email at the bottom of the range and we send a sign-in link. You can come back to the same estimate from any device — no password, ever.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Amber** — assumed. A typical size, a surface we guessed, an answer the assistant filled in. Tap to confirm or change.
- **Cyan** — confirmed by you.
- **Guide / Detailed / Confirmed** — how tight the range is; it tightens as you confirm rooms.

## If something goes wrong
- "Online estimates are nearly here" — the online estimate is switched off at the moment. Leave your number and we call you.
- The address will not look up — type the suburb and postcode in the two fields that appear; that is all the price needs.
- The range disappears after an edit — some answers need a person (a large job, outside work, damage we cannot see). The screen says why and offers a visit or a call.
- Anything else: ring the number at the top of every screen.
