## What this is for
Trade clients (agencies, builders, property managers) get a workspace with their properties, approvals, invoices and a Team screen. Trade access is only ever set up here in the office, never by the client themselves.

## Before you start
You need a staff login, and the trade client's email address. That email becomes their username.

## Steps
1. Go to **Settings → Trade accounts**.
2. Under **Create a trade account**, type their **Email**, a **Company / contact name**, a **Phone** (optional) and a **Starting password** (8 or more characters).
3. Press **Create trade account**.
4. Hand them the password yourself. They can change it any time in their profile.
5. To give trade access to a customer who is already on file, use **Grant an existing customer**: search their email and press **Grant trade**.
6. To take it away again, press **Remove trade access** beside them under **Current trade accounts**.

## What the messages mean
- **Done — … can sign in at /account with that password** — a new login and a new trade account were made, and they own it.
- **… already had a login, so their password is untouched** — the email already signed in to Paint Group. The trade account was made (or found) by that email and they were added as its owner; they keep signing in as before.

The account is found by **email only**. A phone number that's already on another customer's account (your own mobile, the office number) is just saved on the new account; it never picks up that other customer's account.

## If something goes wrong
- **The trade workspace shows a different email or name than you typed.** Before 11 Oct 2026 a matching phone could pick up another customer's account. Tell the developer; it needs repairing, not re-creating.
- **"The password needs at least 8 characters."** Make the starting password longer.
