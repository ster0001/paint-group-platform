## What this is for
When you pay your deposit, you see a short thank-you that confirms your start date is held. Then you're taken back to your invoice, marked paid.

## Before you start
Nothing. It happens on its own after the card payment.

## Steps
1. Pay your deposit from the invoice link we sent you.
2. You'll see **Thank you — your deposit is paid** for a moment.
3. You're taken back to your invoice. Press **Back** if you'd rather not wait.

## What the colours and labels mean
The page shows our name, one line and a **Back** button.

## If something goes wrong
- **The page didn't move on.** Press **Back**, or open the invoice link from your email again.

## Related
- Your invoice and receipts are in your account, under Documents.
