## What this is for
When Paint Group is painting your property you follow the job from your phone: a text when the team arrives, an update with photos at the end of each day, and a text when it is finished with your walkthrough time. The phone on your estimate shows you what that looks like, using your own address, the rooms on your quote and the photos we took when we came to see the property.

## Before you start
Nothing. It plays on its own when you scroll to it, on any phone, tablet or computer.

## Steps
1. Open your estimate from the link we sent you.
2. Scroll to just below **Scope of works, item by item**. The phone starts when it comes into view: a text arrives, the job opens, and the updates appear one by one.
3. Press **↻ Play again** to watch it again. The hero button **See how you'll follow the job** and the link in **What happens when you accept** both jump to it.
4. Accepting the estimate is unchanged: **Accept estimate** at the top or bottom.

## What the labels mean
- **Example of your live updates** and **For illustration only** — the days, times, messages and the painter shown are examples. Your real schedule and your lead painter are confirmed when you book.
- **Before · your photo** — that photo is one of the ones we took at your property. We never show you another customer's photos here.
- **In progress** / **Ready for walkthrough** — how the real portal shows where your job is up to.

## If something goes wrong
- **It does not play.** If your device is set to reduce motion, the phone shows the finished state instead of animating. Otherwise scroll so the whole phone is on screen.
- **A room name or the address looks wrong.** It is taken from your estimate, so tell us and we will fix the estimate itself.
- **You would rather talk to someone.** Use **Ask a question** on the estimate or call the number on it.
