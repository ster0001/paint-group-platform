## What this is for
Paint Home is for homeowners who want to do the legwork themselves. You price your painting online, from your phone, and a person confirms the price on one short call. There is no salesperson in your lounge room.

## Before you start
Your address, and a rough idea of what needs painting: which rooms, or the outside. Photos help but are not required.

## Steps
1. Open painthome.com.au and type your address into the box at the top. Pick it from the list if you can (your suburb and postcode then come along); typed on its own is fine too. Choose **Inside** or **Outside** under it.
2. Press **See my price**. The estimator opens and asks about your home: what is being painted, roughly how big, the condition.
3. Answer the quick questions: the kind of home, bedrooms, what is being painted, the condition. Nothing about coats or products.
4. A price range appears on screen. It tightens as you answer. For an outside job the low end of the range sits a little further under the price, so there is room for it to come in cheaper. Save it and it is yours to come back to.
5. Press **Confirm my price. Book a call**. One of our people calls you, checks the details, and the price is then set. There is no site visit for most Paint Home jobs.
6. Pick a start date from what is available. You will know who is coming before it is confirmed.

## Your emails, texts and account
Everything we send about a Paint Home job comes from Paint Home: the estimate, the invoice, the receipt, the booking confirmation and the walk-through invite. Links open on painthome.com.au. Your invoice names Paint Home and shows the legal line *Enlvn Pty Ltd trading as Paint Home*. If you have had work done through Paint Group before, the same login shows all of your jobs in one place, each marked with where it came from.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Powered by Paint Group** (under the reviews and in the footer) — Paint Home is run by Paint Group. The reviews you see are Paint Group's Google reviews, and the two-year guarantee and the public liability insurance are the same documents.
- **Enlvn Pty Ltd trading as Paint Home** — the legal entity behind both names.

## If something goes wrong
- **My job is larger than a few rooms.** Enter the address anyway. If it is more than can be priced from your phone, the price screen says so and offers a site visit instead of a call. Everything you have entered is kept; the Paint Group team, who look after larger jobs, will be in touch.
- **I would rather talk first.** The phone number is at the top of every page and in the bar at the bottom on a phone. Mon to Fri, 8am to 5pm.
