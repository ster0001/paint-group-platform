## What this is for
You cannot get into your portal: the password is not working or you have forgotten it. The office can fix it in two ways, whichever suits you.

## Before you start
- Ring or message the office and tell them which email you signed up with.

## Steps
1. Ask the office for a **reset link** or a **new password**.
2. If they email you a link, open it on your phone. It signs you in and shows **Choose a new password**.
   - Type the password you want, at least 8 characters, then type it again.
   - Press **Save password**. Your portal opens.
3. If they set a password for you instead, they will read it out. Sign in at the usual sign-in page with your email and that password. You can ask for a reset link afterwards to change it to one of your own.
4. Next time, sign in with your email and the new password.

## If something goes wrong
- **The link says it has expired or was already used.** Links last an hour and work once. Ask the office for a fresh one.
- **"Those two don't match."** The two boxes are different. Type the password again in both.
- **"Use at least 8 characters."** Make the password longer.
- **Still stuck?** Ring the office. They can set a password for you on the spot.
