## What this is for
The Estimates page is where the office finds any estimate: one being built, one sent and waiting, one the customer opened, one won or lost. Each row tells you how many times the customer opened it and whether they came back to the online quote.

## Before you start
Nothing. The page opens on **All estimates**.

## Steps
1. Open **Estimates** from the menu. The row of chips at the top is the six views: **All estimates · Pipeline · Online now · Work queue · Funnel · Campaigns › Automations**. All estimates is the one pressed.
2. Under All estimates are the same tabs as before — **Waiting, All, Draft, Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Wizard** — and the Paint Group / Paint Home filter on the right.
3. Type in the search box to narrow the tab to a name, address, title or estimate number. The list stays newest first.
4. Tap a row's title to open the estimate.
5. On a phone, swipe the chip row sideways to reach the views past Online now.

## The views
- **Pipeline** — every open estimate and every priced wizard session, in a lane for the stage it has reached: Range seen, no action → Needs help → Tightening → Tightened, no action → Visit requested → Visit booked → Call requested → Visit / call done → Estimate sent → Estimate viewed → Question asked → Revised. Each lane shows how many and what they are worth. Cards move on their own as things happen; there is nothing to drag. On a phone, swipe across the lanes.
- **Online now** — Online now (in the wizard in the last five minutes), Needs help, Came back after dropping out (today), Priced, no request, and Dropped today (anonymous people who left without giving details, shown by suburb).
- **Work queue** — everything waiting on someone about an estimate: confirmations, quotes to chase, a visit done with no estimate sent. Out of office hours a yellow line says call-backs and help requests wait until the office opens.
- **Funnel** — says "the next build" for now, with a link to the home dashboard.
- **Campaigns › Automations** — opens CRM › Campaigns until the Automations section is built.

## Pipeline cards
1. Tap a card to open the estimate (or the wizard session, if there is no estimate yet).
2. Tap **⋯** on a card to mark the customer **hot**, **warm** or **cold**, or snooze them for 1, 3 or 7 days (or type a number of days). Both are about the customer, so all their estimates change together.
3. A snoozed customer is off the board until the snooze ends; the line above the lanes says how many are snoozed.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Left edge of a card** — red hot, amber warm, blue cold, grey not set.
- **Viewed 3×** (cyan outline) — the customer opened the estimate three times. One visit is one view: a refresh in the same tab is not counted again; opening the link again in a new tab is.
- **Came back ×2** (green) — the customer left the online quote and came back to it twice. Coming back is the strongest sign they are still interested.
- **about $6,400** — the wizard's estimate of value; a plain **$6,400** is the estimate's price.
- **Paint Home** (dark slate and cyan) / **Paint Group** (grey) — which website the enquiry came through.
- **Last sent — · Next due —** — automated messages, once the automations are built.

## Who sees which view
Pipeline and Funnel can be switched off for a login: Settings → Company → Staff logins, untick **Estimates › Pipeline** or **Estimates › Funnel**. That person no longer sees the chip, and the link takes them to All estimates.

## If something goes wrong
- **No "Viewed" tag on an estimate** — the customer has not opened the link yet, or the estimate has not been sent.
- **A view says it could not be loaded** — reload the page. If it keeps happening, tell Tom; the message on the page is what he needs.
