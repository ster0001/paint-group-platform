## What this is for
The Estimates page is where the office finds any estimate: one being built, one sent and waiting, one the customer opened, one won or lost. Each row tells you how many times the customer opened it and whether they came back to the online quote.

## Before you start
Nothing. The page opens on **All estimates**.

## Steps
1. Open **Estimates** from the menu. The row of chips at the top is the six views: **All estimates · Pipeline · Online now · Work queue · Funnel · Campaigns › Automations**. All estimates is the one pressed.
2. Under All estimates are the same tabs as before — **Waiting, All, Draft, Sent, Viewed, Accepted, Declined, Expired, Wizard** — and the Paint Group / Paint Home filter on the right.
3. Type in the search box to narrow the tab to a name, address, title or estimate number. The list stays newest first.
4. Tap a row's title to open the estimate.
5. On a phone, swipe the chip row sideways to reach the views past Online now.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Viewed 3×** (cyan outline) — the customer opened the estimate three times. One visit is one view: a refresh in the same tab is not counted again; opening the link again in a new tab is.
- **Came back ×2** (green) — the customer left the online quote and came back to it twice. Coming back is the strongest sign they are still interested.
- **Paint Home** (dark slate and cyan) / **Paint Group** (grey) — which website the enquiry came through.
- **Pipeline, Online now, Work queue, Funnel** — say "the next build" for now, with a link to where that information already lives (CRM › Pipeline, the Wizard tab, the Waiting tab, the home dashboard).
- **Campaigns › Automations** — opens CRM › Campaigns until the Automations section is built.

## If something goes wrong
- **No "Viewed" tag on an estimate** — the customer has not opened the link yet, or the estimate has not been sent.
- **The list says it could not be loaded** — reload the page. If it keeps happening, tell Tom; the message on the page is what he needs.
