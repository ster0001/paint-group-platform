## What this is for
Paint Group kept its earlier quotes in another system. When you sign in to your account with the email address you used then, those quotes and jobs are listed under **My estimates** so you can see what was quoted, what was accepted and when.

## Before you start
Sign in with the same email address Paint Group had for you. The sign-in link arrives by email; there is no password.

## Steps
1. Open your account and tap **My estimates**.
2. Each earlier quote lists with its address, its status (accepted, declined, sent) and **From our records** with the month it was sent.
3. These records do not open — they were made before this website — so there is no document to view. If you need a copy of an earlier quote, ask the office.
4. A job that was signed before cutover but not yet painted opens normally: tap it to see the estimate, the price you accepted and, once it is booked, the dates.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Accepted** — you went ahead with the quote.
- **Declined** — you told us not to proceed.
- **Sent** — the quote is still open.
- **From our records** — a record from before this website, shown for your reference.

## If something goes wrong
- **A quote is missing** — it may have been under a different email address. Contact the office with the address of the property and they will link it to your account.
- **A record looks wrong** — contact the office; nothing in your account changes what you were quoted or paid.
