## What this is for
Every customer Paint Group quoted or painted since May 2025 is now in the CRM, brought across from Airtable with their estimates, jobs and the notes the office kept. The jobs that were already signed in PaintScout when the platform went live sit in the schedule as live jobs. Use this page when you open a record and see "Imported from Airtable", or when a job in the tray carries an "Airtable:" note.

## Before you start
The import runs once, at cutover. Nothing here is edited by hand afterwards — a customer record you change stays changed; the import never overwrites the office's temperature, state, tags or follow-up.

## Steps
1. Open **CRM → Customers** and search a name, phone or company. An agency's agents are found by the agency name too (for example "Kay & Burton"), because the company sits on each agent's record.
2. On the record, the line under the name says **Imported from Airtable** when the customer came across. The company, if any, sits beside it.
3. Read **Estimates**: each imported quote lists with its status and amount. Tap one and it opens as a card — status, totals, level of finish, dates and a **PaintScout ↗** link to the actual quote. It has no scope here and cannot be edited or re-priced; the PaintScout link is the document.
4. Read **Jobs before the platform**: every Airtable project, with its dates, estimated and actual hours, painter count, contractor offer, invoice total and its PaintScout link. These are records only — they never appear on the schedule board.
5. Read **Everything, in order**: the timeline carries the Airtable notes on their real dates, with "— Tom" or "— Robyn" where the note was initialled, and the sent, accepted, declined, lapsed, started and completed events on the dates they happened.
6. On **Estimates**, the source chips under the tabs gain **Imported history**. Imported quotes live behind that chip and stay out of **All**, so the list you scan every morning is still the list you can act on.
7. On **Today**, an imported open quote raises a follow-up card only when the customer is hot or warm. A cold or unrated one waits until someone touches it.
8. Open **PC → Schedule**. The 35 jobs signed in PaintScout sit in the **Unscheduled** tray as ordinary jobs, each with a note beginning **Airtable:** — the dates and painter Airtable had planned, and whether that painter had accepted. Send the offer to the painter the note names, exactly as for any other job. No offer, email or text was sent by the import.
9. Open one of those estimates from the tray or the record. The amber strip says **Imported from PaintScout** with links to the quote and the work order. The price is the signed price to the cent; every surface and line carries its PaintScout hours and price, and the rate card never re-prices it. To change the scope, use **Revision → Working scope** as for any accepted job: the diff prices the change and drafts the variation for signature.
10. A job that arrives through the Airtable feed after cutover carries **Hours to confirm** on its strip and a card on Today: open the work order and type each area's hours from the PaintScout work order before offering it.
11. Every job that came across signed — the 35 and any that arrives through the feed — has a **draft deposit** under Invoicing for half the accepted price. Nothing is sent until you issue it. If the customer already paid PaintScout, issue it and record the payment; if no deposit was taken, void it. The final invoice at sign-off then bills the balance.

## What the colours and labels mean
- **Imported from Airtable** (grey, on the record) — the customer came from the import; their details are real, their marketing permissions are unknown.
- **Amber strip** on an estimate — imported from Airtable or PaintScout; the strip carries the links and says whether hours are still to confirm.
- **Lapsed** — an Airtable quote that had gone cold for more than 90 days.
- **Airtable:** on a tray card — what Airtable had planned for the job; a staff note, not the painter's.

## If something goes wrong
- **A customer appears twice** — the import attached history to an existing email rather than creating a second record; if two records still exist, use the duplicate banner on the record to merge them.
- **A PaintScout link is missing** — Airtable held no link for 23 projects; they are listed in the import's exceptions file. There is nothing to add on screen.
- **A tray job shows the wrong hours or price** — open the estimate; the figures are the PaintScout ones. Raise it with Tom rather than editing, because the customer signed that price.
- **A follow-up card appears for a customer who was never chased** — the card is raised from the imported sent date; log the call or dismiss the card as usual.
