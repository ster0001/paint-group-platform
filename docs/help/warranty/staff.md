## What this is for
Every job Paint Group completes carries a two-year workmanship warranty. Since 19 September 2026 the terms are approved and published, so customers read them **before** they accept, not only afterwards. This page is what the office needs to answer a customer's question without ringing anyone.

## What we promise
- **Two years from the day the customer signs off.** Not from the start date, not from the invoice — the sign-off date, which is what the warranty card in their account shows.
- **Workmanship only**: peeling, flaking, blistering, cracking, runs, sags, misses, and premature breakdown where the preparation or the number of coats was not carried out. We supply labour and materials for warranty work at no charge.
- **A repair you cannot see.** Where a touch-up would leave a visible patch we repaint to the nearest natural break, such as the corner of a wall. This is a written commitment, so price it in when you plan warranty work.
- **We acknowledge a claim within 2 business days**, and where an inspection is needed we offer a time **within 10 business days**. These are published promises now, so treat them as a deadline.

## What it does not cover
Ordinary wear and tear; damage by anyone else, including other trades; building movement and substrate cracking; moisture from outside the paint film (leaks, rising damp, failed sealant, condensation); timber decay or rust in the substrate unless we were scoped to treat it; fading and chalking of exteriors within the manufacturer's expectations; surfaces painted at the customer's request against our recommendation; and paint the customer supplied — though our workmanship in applying it is still covered. If the paint product itself is faulty, the manufacturer's warranty applies and we help them claim.

## It does not transfer
The warranty is personal to the customer named on the estimate. If they sell the house, it does not pass to the buyer, and it cannot be assigned to anyone else. If a new owner rings, that is a quote, not a claim. Nothing in that clause takes away anyone's rights under the Australian Consumer Law, so never tell a caller they "have no rights" — tell them the warranty does not transfer.

## Where the customer sees it
- **On their estimate**, before they accept: a card in the trust row beside public liability and the SWMS, and a **Your two-year workmanship warranty** section with the full terms folded behind "The warranty in full".
- **On the printed or PDF quote**: a Two-year workmanship warranty block above the paint list.
- **In their account** once the job is signed off: the warranty card with a live countdown, the **Warranty certificate (PDF)**, the full terms, and the **Report an issue** form. Their account is reached from the Home screen → **Your documents**.

## Can I turn it off for one job?
No, and that is deliberate. Tom's ruling on 19 September 2026 was "keep the 2 year workmanship warranty active for everybody" — there is no per-estimate switch and no per-job term, including for rental touch-ups and commercial work. If a job genuinely cannot carry it, that is a conversation with Tom, not a setting.

## If something goes wrong
- **The terms show a DRAFT watermark** in a customer's account. The approval flag is off: Settings → Documents → the warranty terms approval tick. Flag it immediately — customers are seeing an unapproved-looking document.
- **The terms show no ABN or business address.** Clause 1 renders the entity details from **Settings → Invoicing entity**. A warranty against defects is required to name the warrantor, so fill those in rather than leaving them blank.
- **A customer reports an issue** from their account: it lands in the PC console's queue. Phone and email claims must be entered there too, or the 2-day clock is running with nobody watching it.
