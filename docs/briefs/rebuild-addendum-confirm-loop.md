# Rebuild plan v2 — addendum 1: the confirm loop + cupboard painting

Commit to `docs/briefs/` beside `wizard-rebuild-plan-v2.md`. Reference mockups: `design/reference/customer-review-confirm-mockup.html` (interior), `design/reference/customer-review-confirm-exterior-v2-sides.html` (exterior — supersedes the element-grouped exterior layout). This extends **R4** (mockup parity); it changes the editor's completion model and adds one substrate family.

## 0 · Exterior is built BY SIDES of the house

The exterior editor is organised as **elevations — Front (street), Left side, Right side, Back** — each its own confirmable area holding its substrates (weatherboards, windows with counts and sizes, doors, fascias & gutters, eaves, downpipes), followed by Freestanding extras (fence, pergola — things not on a wall), Condition & access, the windows/doors totals check, and the sweep. Reasons this is the layout: it's how the work order reads ("Front elevation — weatherboards, 3 windows (1 large), entry door, fascias & gutters"), how a painter walks the job, and how partial repaints are naturally expressed. Each side's first required question is **"Are we painting this side?"** — "No, skip this side" marks it NOT PAINTING (grey, dashed on the plan-view), removes its value, and it appears on the quote as an explicit exclusion; extent options (whole house / front only) are thereby replaced by side selection, which is clearer and maps 1:1 to work-order sections. The visual panel is a **top-down house outline** whose four edges tint amber → cyan (or grey for skipped) as sides confirm. Per-side "+ Add a surface" adds to that side specifically, so additions land in the right work-order section. The estimate/work-order rendering groups exterior scope by elevation in this same order.

## 1 · The confirm loop (replaces "everything editable, nothing owed")

The editor becomes a **room-by-room confirmation walk**. Rules:

- Every room/area starts **amber** ("to confirm") — card border, status pill, and its shape on the plan.
- Each room carries its questions inline: size looks-right/adjust (required), its surface tiles, and any room-specific required question (cupboards — §2). Required questions show a REQUIRED tag that flips to ✓ when answered.
- **Confirm [room] ✓** validates its required questions — unanswered = shake + toast naming the gap, never a silent pass. On success: card and plan shape turn **cyan**, pill reads CONFIRMED ✓, and the next unconfirmed room auto-opens (the walk guides itself).
- Header carries the loop state: progress bar + "N of M confirmed", top status pill amber "IN REVIEW" → cyan "ESTIMATE CONFIRMED ✓" only when **all** rooms AND the final sweep are confirmed.
- The final item is always the **missed-rooms sweep**: "Hallways are the ones floorplans miss most — and they make the biggest difference to the price. Laundries, toilets and studies go missing too." Chips: **Hallway (listed first — it's the highest-cost commonly-missed area)**, laundry, toilet, study, garage, other room — each added room appears as a new amber card in the loop — or "No — that's everything ✓". Required like any room.
- **Doors & windows check card** — a required loop item between the rooms and the sweep: "We make it N doors and M windows across the house — is that right?" with live totals computed from the current tiles (updating as steppers change). "That's right ✓" satisfies it; "Something's off — I'll adjust" directs to the room steppers and keeps it unconfirmed until counts are re-affirmed. Nothing completes with unexamined door/window counts.
- **Window sizes are customer-editable** (supersedes the staff-only scope of fix A6): every window tile carries an S / M / L control (default M), applying the 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2 multipliers with a delta toast. The wizard still writes medium; multipliers stay Settings values; builder/capture behaviour from A6 is unchanged. **Windows are groups, not a single row:** a room or side can hold multiple window tiles, each with its own count and size — "+ Add a surface" always offers "**More windows — a different size**" (repeatable), so "2 medium + 1 large on the front" is expressed naturally. The totals check counts across all window groups on included areas, and the work order lists them per size ("3 windows — 2 M, 1 L").
- **Custom-surface prompt:** adding a surface we don't have on file responds with — "Thanks — we've added it, and we'll confirm this area on the site visit." An estimate containing any custom surface routes to the **human sign-off tier** (an unpriced area can never be accepted at a fixed price), and the prompt sets that expectation at the moment it's created.
- **Room sizes display as length × width** ("4.0 × 3.5 m") everywhere customer-facing, never m² — customers can pace out a wall; nobody knows their lounge's square metreage. m² stays the internal unit (tree, pricing, capture, builder are unchanged); the customer size-adjust control takes L and W and derives m². Typical-size defaults come from `wizard-business-inputs.md`, which already stores dimensions.
- **Customers can correct room measurements.** "Adjust it" on the size question opens inline L × W inputs (metres, decimal keypad, "pace it out — near enough is fine") with an Update button. Updating: recomputes the room's areas via the engine, reprices, replaces the size everywhere it displays (question + card header, suffixed "updated by you"), flips the value's provenance to human_confirmed, and satisfies the size question. Sanity clamps (1–15 m per side) with a gentle toast, never a block — the customer knows their house better than the floorplan does; a wildly changed size additionally raises a quiet flag in the estimator prep pack ("size corrected from plan: 3.2→5.8 m") so big corrections get human eyes at review.
- **Exterior sides carry a required L × H measurement question**: "This side's about 12 m long × 2.6 m high — sound right?" with Looks right / Adjust (two inline metre inputs; helper copy: pace the length, single storey is usually 2.4–2.7 m). Both dimensions are the customer's to state or correct — height is no longer read-only. Updating reprices the side through the engine: wall surfaces from the new L × H area, run items (fascias & gutters, eaves) from the new length. **"Not sure" in either field is accepted** — satisfies the question, notes "we'll measure", widens the range until the visit. A painted side cannot confirm without its dimensions answered.
- **Wall surfaces come from the wizard's "what's the house made of?" answer — the editor shows only those.** Each side's walls grid is seeded with just the substrate(s) the customer chose in the wizard (e.g. weatherboard → one tile at 100%). The other wall types on file (render, painted brick, stucco — data-driven from the rate card) live in **"+ Add a surface"** as "+ Render — wall surface" chips; adding one drops a new wall tile into the side **with the "% of wall" chips inside it** (same pattern as window sizes), pre-set to 25% and **auto-balancing** — the largest existing surface gives up the share so the side stays at 100%, with a toast explaining what moved. The live total line and the confirm-requires-100% rule are unchanged. Pricing: side wall value = L × H wall area × Σ(share × substrate rate factor); the work order reads per side ("Front — weatherboard 75%, render 25%").
- **Every room offers "+ Add a surface"**: opens the FULL surface catalogue on file (all rate-card interior surfaces, data-driven per fix A2 — never a curated subset), filtered to what the room doesn't already have; tapping adds a priced tile with the delta toast. Below the catalogue, a free-text "Something else?" adds a **custom surface**: rendered as an amber dashed ⚑ tile, recorded on the estimate, visible in the estimator's prep pack — and **never auto-priced** (custom = we price it with you; a room containing a custom surface can still be confirmed, but the estimate carries an open amber item that the desk review/visit resolves).
- The sticky CTA is **disabled** ("Confirm all rooms to continue — N of M") until the loop completes, then becomes Accept / book-the-visit per the ladder. Acceptance and sign-off tiers sit *behind* full confirmation, always.
- Accuracy interplay: each room confirmation flips its values to human_confirmed (score rises as rooms turn cyan); the loop completing is what earns the tightest range the bands allow.

Why this exists: it fixes "the AI doesn't always pick up all areas" twice — nothing completes with an unexamined room, and nothing completes without the customer explicitly answering "is anything missing?". Colour semantics use the locked palette: amber = awaiting, cyan = confirmed (Tom's orange/blue).

## 2 · Cupboard painting (new substrate family + required questions)

- New interior substrate family: **cabinetry**, priced per door/drawer front (new rate items: kitchen cupboard front, robe door, vanity door — Settings-priced like everything else; indicative defaults in the mockup: $85 / $140 / $95).
- Room-type rules attach a required cupboard question where relevant: Kitchen → "Are we painting the kitchen cupboards?" (yes → stepper for doors & drawer fronts, default 14, plus the spray-finish note); Bedrooms with robes → "Paint the built-in robe doors?" (stepper); Bathroom/laundry → "Paint the vanity/cupboard?" (stepper). Rooms without cabinetry get no cupboard question.
- "Yes" adds priced lines with the delta toast; "No" is a recorded answer (not an omission) — consistent with the R1.2 principle that nothing the customer told us about contributes $0 silently.
- Wizard page 2 gains **Cupboards & cabinetry** in the interior surface list; ticking it seeds the room questions. Capture and builder get the same cabinetry surfaces (staff side) so parity holds.

*e2e for this addendum:* walk all rooms → each turns cyan only after required answers; skip a required question → shake + named gap; add Laundry in the sweep → new amber card appears and total moves; complete everything → header flips, CTA enables; kitchen "yes, 14 fronts" adds the priced lines and the work order shows the cabinetry scope.
