# Work order completion loop — workflow (Phase 1)

Companion mockup: `design/reference/work-order-lifecycle-mockup.html`. Builds on WO v1 (exists) and the shipped scheduling/contractor phases; this module owns everything from booking-confirmed to signed-off-and-closed. The completion report is not a feature — it is the by-product of the stages below.

## The shape
Seven gated stages, four lanes (Contractor on site · Project Coordinator console · Customer portal · System events). Same rule as the customer confirm loop: **a stage cannot be passed until its gate is met**, and the gate is a visible checklist — that is how nothing gets missed. Every action writes an event (who/what/when/photo) to the job timeline; the customer portal and the completion report are both just renderings of that timeline.

## Stages & gates

**1 · CREATED (System → PC).** WO generated from the accepted estimate snapshot: scope grouped by area (interior) / elevation (exterior, "Front — 12 × 2.6 m, weatherboard 75% / render 25%, 3 windows (2 M, 1 L)…"), **level-of-finish banner + per-surface standards labels**, materials list, equipment list (from the estimate's equipment/pass-through lines), exclusions (incl. NOT PAINTING sides), variations section (empty). Colours may not exist yet — the WO shows an amber **"Colours: TBC — consult booked"** chip until the schedule is finalised. *Gate = **ready to offer**, not ready to start: PC ticks scope-matches-estimate + finish-level-and-standards-shown.* The WO then travels **attached to the booking offer** — the contractor reviews the full scope (colours-pending visible) before accepting the fixed price; acceptance runs through the existing scheduling flow.

**2 · PRE-START (PC, after contractor acceptance).** Checklist: **colour schedule finalised** (from the consult — blocks the next item) · materials ordered/allocated (depends on colours) · equipment movements scheduled (each item: source, deliver-by, courier/self, return trigger pre-created) · access + key details recorded · SWMS/induction attached if commercial · QA schedule auto-created when the contractor is inside their first N jobs (⚑ default: day-1 prep check + final check for first 3 jobs) · customer "what to expect" message queued. A job approaching its start date with colours unconfirmed shows amber in the PC console with colours as the named blocker. *Gate: all items ticked. Start date arrives → stage 3.*

**3 · IN PROGRESS (Contractor, phone-first).** Per-surface status ticks — tap cycles **TO DO → PREPPED → DONE (cyan)** — under their area/elevation headings, exactly the WO's structure. **Before photos required per area before its first tick; after photos on area completion.** **Variations — the two-sided flow (decided):** contractor raises via a structured card — category chips (rot / damage / extra scope / customer request) + comment box + **photos required** + optional suggested hours. Office prices it through the engine (never the contractor). Then two approvals in order: (1) **customer approves the priced variation** as a mini-estimate through the same portal/SMS link flow as the original quote; (2) on their yes, **the contractor receives an adjusted offer back** — payment + variation hours × $60 — one tap to accept, reusing the job-offer flow. Both taps logged; only then does the scope merge into the WO (amber → cyan when worked). No work on a variation before both approvals; PC verbal-approval override for urgent blockers, logged as such. **Declined variations are recorded, never deleted** — they appear on the completion report as flagged-and-declined (warranty-dispute protection). Blockers flagged (weather, access, discovered damage). **System drafts a daily customer update from the day's ticks; PC approves/edits/sends** — the PC console highlights any working day with zero ticks (the "painter forgot" catch). *Gate to leave: every surface DONE + before/afters present + no variation awaiting either approval.*

**4 · QA (PC, new-contractor jobs or spot checks).** Scheduled checks come due with reminders; each check is a photo-logged record against the standards labels (pass / fail per item). **Fail appends rectification items into the stage-3 tick list** (amber) and blocks completion until re-checked pass. *Gate: all due checks logged as pass.*

**5 · COMPLETION PREP (Contractor).** Checklist: touch-up sweep done · site clean · rubbish handled (⚑ default self vs courier) · equipment return movements triggered · final after-photo set complete. *Gate: all ticked → "Ready for walkthrough" notifies customer + PC.*

**6 · WALKTHROUGH & SIGN-OFF (Customer, portal).** Area-by-area review: each area **Approve ✓ or Flag something** (pin + photo + note → becomes a rectification item → back to stage 3/5 loop → re-review). When every area is approved: type-to-sign (name, timestamp, device). **Remote sign-off timer (customer not on site):** clock starts only when the full evidence pack (after photos + draft report) is delivered — notification at 0h, reminder at 24h, final notice at 48h stating the deadline plainly, **deemed signed off at 72h residential** (Settings value; commercial per contract, typically 5–7 days). Delivery and *view* events are tracked (a viewed-but-silent walkthrough makes deemed acceptance defensible). Customer can request an extension with one tap ("I'm away until…"), PC approves. Deemed sign-off is framed as payment-due, explicitly **not** a waiver: "anything you find later is still covered by your 2-year warranty" (consumer guarantees survive regardless — the clause accelerates payment, it does not extinguish rights). The rule is disclosed in the quote terms, never sprung at completion. ⚑ final clause wording gets a legal review (ACL / unfair-contract-terms). **Sign-off (typed or deemed) is the master event**: it stamps warranty start (⚑ confirm = sign-off date), fires the final invoice (Phase 2 hook), queues the review request (Phase 5 hook), and **generates the completion report** — scope delivered incl. approved variations *and declined ones noted*, before/afters per area, QA records, colour schedule, warranty certificate — one document, zero writing.

**7 · CLOSED (System/PC).** Equipment returns confirmed · contractor invoice released for payment (their portal flow) · materials reconciliation hook (Phase 6) · job archived to the customer's records with the report. Job P&L snapshot drafted.

## Build rules (unchanged laws)
One tree: the WO renders the estimate snapshot + approved variations — it never holds its own copy of scope. All money server-side (variation pricing through `lib/pricing`; contractor payment adjustments via RPC). Events are append-only. e2e specs run as contractor JWT, PC login and customer token respectively. Reference mockup checklist per PR.

## Who sees what (the mockup shows all lanes only because it's a demo)
Each lane is its own rendered view behind RLS **and** the explicit `view=` contract — never inferred from role (the wizard's payload-by-role bug is why this is written down). **Contractor:** stages 3 & 5, their scope, standards labels, their payment incl. variation adjustments — never margins, customer contact beyond site access, or other contractors' rates. **Customer:** updates, walkthrough, sign-off, completion report — never contractor payments, internal QA workings, or margins. **PC/staff:** everything, plus the console (tick gaps, QA due, timers running). e2e specs run one per lane, as that lane's real credential.
