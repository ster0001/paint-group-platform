# Manual test — employed painters, Session 3 (the employee's portal)

Needs `20270153`, `20270154`, `20270157` applied. Make a test painter an employee and assign them a job from the board (see the S2 script).

1. Sign in as the employee. **Jobs** lists the job with an amber **Tap Accept** chip, **Lead** if they are the lead, the start date and **Time budget: N DAYS · H.H H**. No price anywhere. **Calendar** shows their days in the job colour; no Google Calendar card.
2. Open the job. **You're on this job** shows their days and the time budget; the address is the full address. Tap **Accept — I've seen it** → the card reads "Accepted …". On the board, their block loses its dashed outline.
3. Scroll: no offer bar, no "Request a new start date", no variation prices, no "Invoice this job", no "Share with your crew". The job sheet's stage badge reads **02 Pre-start**; a job still at stage 1 would read **01 Assigned**. No **Payment** section.
4. **Projects → the job** as staff: the rail's first stop reads **01 Assigned**; if it were still at stage 1 the checklist would be titled **Ready to assign**.
5. Tick the office's pre-start list as staff, then as the employee tap **Start the job**. Take a before photo of an area (or insert one), tick a surface **PREPPED** then **DONE**. As staff, **Projects → Updates**: today's draft for the job mentions the ticked area, and the job's event log shows the employee as the actor.
6. As the employee, on the job: **I can't make these days** → reason → **Tell the office**. The card says the office has been told. The assignment is unchanged (board block, dates, lead). As staff, **CRM → Today** (scope everyone): a **Reassign** item "… can't make … — dd/mm–dd/mm" with the reason, action **Reassign** opening the board on those dates.
7. Move their days on the board → the Reassign item clears and the employee's job shows **Tap Accept** again.
8. Help, as the employee: **Help** lists the two employee guides only (assigned jobs; running a job) — no contractor guide about offers or invoices.
