# Paint Home go-live finish: manual walk (26 Nov 2026)

Use the Vercel preview of the PR, on the Paint Home host, signed out. Do each step on a laptop and on a phone.

## Legal and footer
1. Footer → Privacy, Terms, Warranty. Each opens a page titled "… | Paint Home". No footer link goes nowhere.
2. Privacy page: shows hello@painthome.com.au and "Enlvn Pty Ltd trading as Paint Home"; never "Paint Group". Quotation marks are curly, the contact line uses middle dots.
3. Type a nonsense address after the domain. The tab title does not say "Paint Group".

## Chat
4. Tap the chat bubble on the homepage, on /work and on /privacy. The first message reads "Hi — you're talking to Paint Home…" and replies are labelled Paint Home. Nothing says Paint Group.
5. Start an estimate and open the chat in the wizard: the same, and there is no "describe the job" box.
6. On the Paint Group site the chat still says Paint Group.
7. From the staff builder, reply on a Paint Home estimate's chat. The customer's text and email name Paint Home; replying to the email still reaches the office mailbox.

## Send a message
8. In the wizard choose Send a message. Fill in the details, then select the text in a field by dragging and let go outside the panel. The panel stays open with your words in it.
9. Do the same in the message box. Then press once on the dimmed area: it closes.

## Details step
10. Bad email → "Please enter a valid email address, like name@example.com." A landline → "That looks like a landline. We need a mobile number to text your code." The line is on screen without scrolling.
11. The Privacy policy link is above the button and opens /privacy.

## Other

13. Paint Home homepage: no "This month at Paint Home" panel. Paint Group's homepage still has its panel.
