# Manual test — paint on every substrate, no hours on the painter's sheet (21 Oct 2026)

No migration. Deploy, then:

## As the painter (contractor portal, or the share link)
1. Open any booked job → the job sheet. Under each surface name there is a paint line with a swatch: **Dulux Wash&Wear · Low Sheen · Natural White** (or **… · Colour to be confirmed** where no colour is chosen yet). A line with no paint (an allowance, "move furniture") has no paint line.
2. Scroll to **Materials & colours**. Under each paint, small chips say where it goes: **Living room · Walls · 2 coats**, one per substrate.
3. The facts at the top have Customer, Start date, Contractor, Access notes — **no Estimated hours**. No surface has an hours figure on its right; the pill (Not started / Prepped / Done) is alone. No "Condition · extra prep" line. A job with an approved change lists it under **Changes to the scope** with no "~N h".
4. Share with your crew → open the crew link signed out: same paint lines, same chips, no hours.
5. Print / Download PDF: the paint line prints dark, the swatch shows.

## As the office
6. PC Command → job → **Painter's view**: reads as the painter does (no hours).
7. Open the estimate in the builder → the work-order tab: Estimated hours, the Hours column (editable) and the Condition line are all still there. Change an hour, save, reopen the painter's view: still no hours.
8. Requests card for an offered job still shows **Calculated labour hours** — unchanged on purpose; say if that should go too.
