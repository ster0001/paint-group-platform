How this tab works · 30 seconds
  1. The tray is everything that has to move next week — paint per brand, materials per line, gear, rubbish — built from the jobs. Amber = not yet planned. Anything can also be added from scratch.
  2. Drag a chip onto a person's day. Supplier lane = delivered (you place the order). A painter's lane = they pick it up or move it. Felipe's and Tom's lanes are in hours. Tick Ordered and the painter is told.
  3. Nearby on a day means there are chips close to where that person already is. Day turns a person's drops into a numbered run plan; Map shows every job as an icon — it changes to the movement icon when that job still needs something — and lets you drag the day's stops into order.

Ticking Ordered is what alerts the painter — "ready at Dulux Preston Mega from 6am Tue, order PG-0092 under Paint Group" — or tells them a delivery is coming. Client notice is off unless you turn it on for that delivery.

Every job is one icon. It turns amber and shows a paint / materials / gear / rubbish icon when that job still needs something planned or ordered — so you can see where action is needed. Numbers are the selected person's order for the day. Google Maps underneath in the build.
