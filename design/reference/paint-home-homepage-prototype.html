    Common questions.

    Who is Paint Home?

    Qualified local painters for apartments, units, townhouses and smaller homes across Melbourne, inside and out. Ten years of residential painting, a two-year workmanship guarantee and $20M public liability insurance. Jobs this size don't need a site visit, so we price online and confirm by phone. Paint Home is powered by Paint Group. Enlvn Pty Ltd trading as Paint Home.

    What happens after I enter my address?

    You answer a few questions about the property — what's being painted, roughly how big, the condition. Photos help but aren't required. A price range appears on screen. Save it and it's yours to come back to; nobody rings you unless you ask.

    Is the price on screen my final price?

    It's a real range, and it gets tighter as you answer. The final price is confirmed with you by one of our people on a short call before anything is booked — most Paint Home jobs don't need a site visit. Once it's signed off, that's the number on your invoice; anything extra found on site is priced and approved by you first.

    Who does the painting?

    Our own trusted network — painters who are quality-checked, fully insured and used to working in people's homes. You'll see who's coming in your portal before the start date is locked in.

    My job is larger than a few rooms. Can you still help?

    Enter the address anyway. If it's more than can be priced online — a full heritage house, work needing scaffold, a commercial property — we'll say so and pass you to the right team, with everything you've entered carried across.

    How soon can you start?

    Once your price is signed off, we'll give you the earliest start we can hold for a job that size — smaller interiors are usually quicker than whole-house or exterior work, which depends on the weather. You pick a date that suits from what's available.

    Do I need to be home?

    Not while the painting happens — we'll agree access with you and send photo updates every day. The final walkthrough is done with you there, so we'll book it for a time that works.

    What if something extra is found?

    It's raised as a variation with photos and a price. Nothing extra starts, and nothing extra lands on the invoice, until you've tapped Approve.

    When do I pay?

    A deposit when your price is signed off, and the balance after you've walked the job with us and approved it — not before.

    What does the guarantee cover?

    Our workmanship, for two years from sign-off. If the finish fails because of how it was applied, we come back and fix it. The warranty and our $20M public liability certificate sit in your portal.
