    HOW WE'LL PAINT EACH SURFACE

    Worked out from your answers

    This is the bit most quotes hide. Each line is what your painter will actually do. Change anything that isn't right.

    Walls

    New colour. Fill nail holes and hairline cracks, light sand, spot-prime the fills, then two coats of low-sheen.

    Ceilings and cornices

    White again. One fresh coat of flat ceiling white over a sound surface. Two if we're covering marks or a colour.

    Skirtings, architraves and door frames

    Same white. Sand and clean, fill any dents, then two coats of water-based enamel.

    Are they shiny at the moment — a gloss finish?
    Old oil-based gloss needs a bonding primer first

    Doors

    As the trims. Both sides, edges and frame. 6 doors across the house so far.

    Windows

    Timber, casement. Sand, fill, two coats to frames and sashes. Glass edges cut in by hand.

    Every job gets two coats by default unless a line above says otherwise. Bold accent colours sometimes need a third — if yours does, we'll tell you before we start that area, never after.
